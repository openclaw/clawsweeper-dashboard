## Summary

Keep open. Current-main production runs show that known post-apply outcomes are still converted into generic publisher failures, causing deterministic retries that saturate the publication lane and delay otherwise completed review comments. The merged stale-preflight fix at https://github.com/openclaw/clawsweeper/pull/634 addresses an adjacent branch but does not cover these post-apply outcomes.

## What This Changes

Prevent known non-applied exact-review publication outcomes from failing generically and repeatedly reoccupying publisher capacity.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a typed post-apply publication disposition that terminally completes proven stale or no-op artifacts, defers retryable guards with bounded backoff, preserves unexpected states as hard failures, and guarantees that still-current review comments eventually publish.

## Solution Assessment
