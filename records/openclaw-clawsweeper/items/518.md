## Summary

Keep this PR open: the overall recovery design is well-scoped, but the queue guard only rejects recovery events while ordinary work is pending, allowing a recovery revision to supersede an ordinary item while it is leased and schedule another unnecessary review. The submitted validation is test and CI evidence rather than a live failed-shard recovery run.

## What This Changes

Routes failed review-shard recovery through the durable exact-review queue, makes recovery review-only and one-shot, preserves target branches, bounds dispatch retries, and adds workflow and queue coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Ignore stale recovery deliveries whenever ordinary work is pending or actively leased, preserve any newer ordinary retry revision, add the leased-overlap regression case, and provide a redacted live Actions transcript showing one review-only recovery with no direct workflow dispatch or verdict routing.
