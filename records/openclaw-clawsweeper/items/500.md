## Summary

The exact-version suppression design is fail-open and the two earlier acknowledgement-body races are addressed, but the live head still lets an older duplicate delivery remove the shared processing reaction after a newer edited delivery has started. Because this is a member-authored draft and the remaining defect is narrow, it should stay open for repair rather than cleanup.

## Best Possible Solution

After terminal acknowledgement convergence, make reaction cleanup version-safe—for example delete and immediately revalidate the authenticated source version, restoring the reaction and resuming normal routing on drift—and add a deterministic edit-between-check-and-delete regression test.
