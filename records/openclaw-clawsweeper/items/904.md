## What This Changes

This PR moves state-materializer concurrency from the workflow to its draining job and adds a short privileged job that cancels older scheduled runs which have not begun draining.

## System Context

The state materializer turns durable repair-state append records into worker dispatches and result publication. Scheduled and manually dispatched triggers enter the workflow; only the materialization job should serialize draining, while the new preflight job removes redundant scheduled waiters.
