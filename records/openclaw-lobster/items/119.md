## Summary

This PR addresses real cancellation and resume-replay gaps with strong after-fix evidence, but it still does not prevent an already-aborted workflow from dispatching ordinary shell/command steps and can persist a cancelled GitHub-monitor snapshot after its subprocess returns.

## What This Changes

The branch propagates parent cancellation through selected external commands, halts direct and workflow pipelines after cancellation, and consumes post-start resume state to prevent replaying completed side effects.

## Best Possible Solution

Add cancellation checks at the workflow step-dispatch and monitor-state-write boundaries, with focused regressions proving that pre-aborted non-pipeline workflow steps never start and cancelled monitor runs do not persist a snapshot.
