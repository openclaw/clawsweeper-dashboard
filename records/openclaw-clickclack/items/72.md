The PR lets message authors and workspace owners soft-delete messages and adds eligible delete controls and deleted-message placeholders to channel and thread views.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve author self-deletion, permit owner moderation for workspace channels, and require direct-conversation membership for DM deletion unless maintainers explicitly adopt and document a broader owner privacy policy; cover both stores, the HTTP layer, and visible browser outcomes.
