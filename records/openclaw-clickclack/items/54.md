This PR should remain open because the current upload visibility contract prevents ordinary members from loading a workspace icon uploaded by someone else, and workspace deletion removes upload rows without deleting their stored objects. Represent the icon as a validated upload belonging to the workspace, grant read access only through that workspace-icon reference, clean up or reconcile stored upload objects when icons are replaced or workspaces are deleted, and land the rebased source with equivalent SQLite/Postgres and multi-user HTTP coverage.
