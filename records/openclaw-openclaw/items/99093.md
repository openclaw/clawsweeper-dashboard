Keep open: current `main` and `v2026.6.11` still have the reported Voice Wake restart paths, direct `installTap` call, and overlapping Talk-resume scheduling, so the iOS crash is not already implemented or superseded.

## What This Changes

Harden iOS Voice Wake so restarting recognition after Talk or other microphone capture cannot abort while reinstalling the AVAudioEngine input tap.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a narrow iOS Voice Wake lifecycle fix: serialize and debounce restart attempts, re-check suppression/external-capture ownership before tap installation, and recover from contended microphone startup without aborting the app.
