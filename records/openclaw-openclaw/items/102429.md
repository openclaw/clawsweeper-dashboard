The dedicated policy seam is justified and successful-path proof is strong, but this P1 delivery/security defect, the unresolved fallback contract, and current conflicts with `main` block merge.

## What This Changes

Adds `messages.operationalReplies` with always, once, redirect, and silent modes across direct dispatch, ACP, follow-ups, heartbeat, session persistence, plugin hooks, configuration, tests, and docs.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep one canonical operational-notice policy, but suppress the source only after a durable operator-owned handoff accepts a redirected notice; then resolve current-main conflicts and refresh exact-head proof for successful redirect, redirect failure, once persistence, and all affected delivery siblings.
