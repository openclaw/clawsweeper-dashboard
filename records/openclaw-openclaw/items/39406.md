Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 provide the boolean `messages.suppressToolErrors` opt-out, but they do not implement the requested normal-chat `all | final | off` delivery policy, and the remaining change needs maintainer product direction because it changes safety behavior for failed mutating actions.

## What This Changes

The issue requests a configurable policy for suppressing transient tool-error warning bubbles after recovered or successful agent turns.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one default-compatible normal-chat tool-error delivery contract, keep current safety behavior as the default, and add `final` or `off` only with schema, docs, payload/progress, and representative channel regression coverage.
