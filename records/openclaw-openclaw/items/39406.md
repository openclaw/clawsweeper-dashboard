## Summary

Keep open: current main still has default-visible tool-error warnings plus an all-off boolean, but not the requested success-aware normal-chat `all | final | off` delivery policy; adding that policy needs maintainer product and safety direction.

## What This Changes

Request a config policy to suppress or defer transient tool-error warnings from user chat when the agent recovers and sends a successful final reply.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one default-compatible normal-chat tool-error delivery contract, keep the safety-preserving `all` behavior as the default, and only add `final` or `off` semantics after maintainers choose the mutation-failure safety rules and require matching schema, docs, payload, progress, and channel coverage.
