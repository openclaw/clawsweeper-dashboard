## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still preserve visible mutating-tool warnings after success-looking replies unless the operator opts into full `messages.suppressToolErrors` suppression, so the requested final-only delivery policy remains unresolved and needs product direction.

## What This Changes

Request a configurable normal-chat tool-error delivery policy such as `all | final | off` so recovered/transient tool failures do not create confusing chat warnings after useful final replies.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one default-compatible normal-chat tool-error delivery contract, keep the safety-preserving `all` behavior as the default, and only add `final` or `off` semantics after maintainers choose the mutation-failure safety rules and require matching schema, docs, payload, progress, and channel coverage.
