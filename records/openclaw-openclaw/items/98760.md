The issue reports that Anthropic recovery retries can rewrite thinking blocks in conversation history, causing repeated provider 400 errors and no delivered assistant response.

## Best Possible Solution

Have a provider/session owner choose the signed-thinking retry policy, then fix the recovery retry so provider-valid latest thinking is preserved or repaired without replaying poisoned completed-turn state.
