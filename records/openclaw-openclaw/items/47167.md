Options:

- **Gate The Public Hook Before Landing (recommended):** Add an explicit operator or plugin permission gate, document payload and retention boundaries, and land the hook only after that contract is visible.
- **Approve Installed-Plugin Trust:** Treat installed plugins as trusted enough to observe skipped inbound content without a new gate, and document that security model clearly.
- **Keep Internal Ingest Only:** Reject or pause the public hook and leave full-fidelity archive plugins dependent on channel-owned or internal integrations for now.
