## Summary

Keep open for a QQBot owner decision: the implementation is clean and convincingly proven, but the 1 MiB ceiling is an undocumented compatibility policy choice that could reject legitimate future gateway events.

## What This Changes

The PR adds a 1 MiB inbound `ws` payload limit to QQBot gateway connections and updates direct and proxied constructor-option tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the bound in the QQBot-owned WebSocket factory, use an authoritative or production-observed event-size ceiling—preferably 16 MiB absent stronger evidence for 1 MiB—and retain direct/proxy plus below/above-boundary transport coverage.
