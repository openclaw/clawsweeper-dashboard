## Summary

Keep open for QQBot owner review: the hardening is technically sound and well proven, but the 1 MiB fail-closed ceiling is not guaranteed by the upstream protocol and could reject legitimate events. Likely related people are cxyhhhhh for the QQBot gateway architecture and hugenshen for recent maintenance of this WebSocket factory.

## What This Changes

The PR gives QQBot gateway WebSocket clients a fixed 1 MiB inbound payload limit and updates direct and proxy-path constructor tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep a plugin-local maxPayload bound, but land an owner-approved ceiling supported by an upstream guarantee or conservative production evidence, with constructor coverage and the existing loopback over-limit proof retained.
