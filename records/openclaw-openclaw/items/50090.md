The issue asks OpenClaw to make community skills reliably discoverable, publishable, watchable, diagnosable, composable, and trustworthy through ClawHub and SKILL.md metadata improvements.

Keep this as an umbrella or split it into maintainer-approved trackers for skill trust, ClawHub publishing, trigger metadata, and agent-pack/composition work under the plugin/ClawHub boundary.
