## Best Possible Solution

Use one server-owned quarantined-only restore transition that refreshes scan data without weakening the apply-time write gate, expose the findings and recovery control in the UI, document the lifecycle and Gateway contract, follow the generated-locale workflow, and prove the complete browser flow before merge.
