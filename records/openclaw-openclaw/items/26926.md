The issue requests inbound images, CSV files, and PDFs in Synology Chat instead of text-only webhook messages.

Confirm Synology's attachment webhook and retrieval contract, then implement plugin-local bounded parsing, safe retrieval/staging, existing inbound media-context population, docs, and focused tests.
