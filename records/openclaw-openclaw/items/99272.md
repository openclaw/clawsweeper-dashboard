Keep this as a maintainer-owned Codex watchdog bug: fix blocker-aware attempt-idle behavior and order-safe blocker tracking, then align wall-clock and active-request timeout policy with https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/89742 and https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/85242.
