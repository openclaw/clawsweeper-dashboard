## What This Changes

The PR makes Google Meet local and paired-node audio bridge shutdown share and await bounded SIGTERM-to-SIGKILL cleanup for active and retired child processes.

## Best Possible Solution

Land one plugin-local termination owner used by every Google Meet command-pair shutdown path, preserving bounded grace periods while ensuring leave and session retirement await active and clearAudio-retired children.
