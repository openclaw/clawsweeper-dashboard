## What This Changes

This PR makes meeting-audio long-poll waits cancel their pending timeout when audio or shutdown wakes them early, and adds timeout-lifecycle regression coverage.

## System Context

Meeting audio is fetched through a node-host long poll used by the realtime audio transport. The waiter completes when audio arrives, the session stops, or its deadline expires, then returns control to the transport polling loop.
