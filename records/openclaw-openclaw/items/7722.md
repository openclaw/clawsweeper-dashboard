## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still expose only the narrower `tools.fs.workspaceOnly` boolean, while configurable allow/deny filesystem policy and exec scope remain unmerged security-sensitive product work. The open candidate PRs at https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/52951 and https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/60981 are useful but competing/partial rather than merged completion.

## What This Changes

Request a configurable filesystem access policy for host-mode agents, with allow/deny path rules covering read, write, edit, and exec-style filesystem effects.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical security/config issue until maintainers choose a versioned `tools.fs` or successor policy with normalized deny-wins path checks, file/media/apply_patch coverage, upgrade docs, and an explicit exec strategy or separate exec follow-up.
