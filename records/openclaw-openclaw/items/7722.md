Options:

- **Define a versioned path policy (recommended):** Specify a `tools.fs` or successor policy with allowed paths, denied paths, normalization, symlink handling, default behavior, docs, tests, and an explicit exec strategy before landing an implementation.

## Solution Assessment

No, the literal `tools.fileAccess` shape is only a plausible direction; the best fix is a maintainer-approved `tools.fs` or successor policy with explicit exec semantics and upgrade docs.
