## Summary

## What This Changes

The issue reports that `openclaw nodes camera snap --device-id <iPhone Continuity Camera> --facing front` captures from the rear camera, the same as `--facing back`.

## Best Possible Solution

Live-test the current macOS companion with a paired iPhone Continuity Camera, then either keep exact `deviceId` targeting with a clear diagnostic/docs limitation or implement a supported Continuity front/back selector with focused tests.
