## Summary

Keep open: current main filters pending plugin approvals through a client-visibility boundary, while existing coverage only proves listing from the requesting client; the detailed v2026.7.1 cross-client report therefore describes credible remaining behavior with no merged fix or canonical open replacement. The repair should wait for an explicit decision about which paired devices may discover and resolve an approval.

## What This Changes

Make pending Skill Workshop approvals discoverable or actionably routed when the initiating channel and the eligible operator client are separate connections.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve the device-bound authorization model, but deterministically enroll eligible paired reviewer devices for each request, replay only their authorized pending records on list/reconnect, notify the initiating surface where approval is required, and cover creation, listing, resolution, and waiter completion across distinct connections.
