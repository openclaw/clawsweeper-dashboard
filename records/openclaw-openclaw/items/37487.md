## Risks / Open Questions

- Proxy credentials could leak through Chrome argv, process listings, logs, config snapshots, or status payloads unless the design uses SecretRef-safe handling, redaction, and a non-argv authentication strategy where possible.
- Browser traffic routed through an explicit proxy weakens OpenClaw's ability to enforce SSRF and private-network policy because the proxy may resolve and connect to the final target outside the guarded process.
- Agent and subagent default-profile binding can expose logged-in cookies, SSO state, or monitored browser sessions without explicit inheritance, authorization, and observability rules.
- Concurrent managed Chromium profiles need launch serialization, cleanup, resource limits, and failure recovery; linked prior art reports multiple Chromium instances can consume several GB of memory.
