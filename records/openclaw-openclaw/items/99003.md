## Best Possible Solution

No: this is the right owner area and now covers both readers, but the PR uses a seconds parser that treats fractional timeoutSeconds as a shortened watchdog even though sessions_send treats fractional values as invalid. The safer fix is to share the validated seconds conversion behavior and cover that contract in both tests.
