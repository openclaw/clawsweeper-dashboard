The issue reports a regression where Codex app-server heartbeat/internal turns are treated as bound to the internal webchat channel, causing default cross-provider policy to silently deny Discord heartbeat notifications.

## Best Possible Solution

Land a scoped Codex resolver fix, such as the approach in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/102232, that removes the internal webchat binding only for heartbeat, cron, and inter-session turns while preserving real WebChat user-turn containment.
