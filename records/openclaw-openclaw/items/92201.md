## Summary

Keep open. Fix embedded Anthropic replay so invalid freshly streamed thinking signatures do not poison sessions and recovery sees and retries raw thinking-signature errors.

Land one maintainer-approved prevention path that stops stale completed-turn Anthropic thinking blocks from bricking replay while preserving latest/active tool-turn semantics, then keep the recovery wrapper as a fallback and close linked duplicates.
