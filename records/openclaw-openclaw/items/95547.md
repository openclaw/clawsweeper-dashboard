## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Define one maintainer-owned Codex/Gateway lifecycle policy that classifies intentional Gateway shutdown separately from unexpected app-server transport failure and gives interrupted channel turns either safe resume, durable final status, or explicit restart-specific recovery guidance.
