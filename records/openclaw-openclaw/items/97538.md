## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to stop WhatsApp durable ingress events from remaining pending and replaying after reconnects, after the related reply-lane release defect was fixed.

## System Context

WhatsApp inbound events enter a durable ingress journal before they are handed to OpenClaw's conversational reply pipeline. Completed events should not replay on reconnect, while the reply-run lifecycle ensures a stalled run cannot indefinitely hold its session lane.
