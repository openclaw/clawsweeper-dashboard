## Reproduction Assessment

## Solution Assessment

Yes. Reusing `shortenHomePath()` is the narrowest maintainable fix for standalone filesystem paths; a hook-local helper would duplicate policy and `shortenHomeInString()` is broader than needed for a single path value.
