## Summary

Keep open: the reported hang is valid, but the unchanged head still has the previously identified P1 defect because error-body reads receive a fresh per-chunk timeout instead of sharing the request’s absolute deadline; the submitted proof also omits a real Urbit ship and a trickling error body.

## What This Changes

Routes Tlon outbound text and media pokes through the shared Urbit channel helper, adds timeout-bounded error-body reads, and adds live-socket regression coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the shared-helper consolidation, but create one absolute deadline before the PUT, propagate its abort signal or remaining budget through headers and bounded error-body consumption, cover a continuously trickling response, and demonstrate both successful and stalled sends against a real Urbit ship.
