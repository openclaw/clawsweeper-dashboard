## Summary

Keep open for maintainer architecture review: current main provides fail-closed tool-call approvals and final outbound hooks, but ordinary delivery hooks deliberately continue after errors or timeouts and therefore do not satisfy the requested trusted final-action security contract.

## What This Changes

Requests a fail-closed trusted preflight policy layer at final external-action boundaries, initially covering outbound message delivery and recovered retries.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a narrow core-generic trusted preflight contract at the durable outbound-message boundary, let explicitly trusted plugins supply policy decisions and optional approval requests, re-run it for recovered intents, and keep ordinary modifying hooks unchanged and fail-open.
