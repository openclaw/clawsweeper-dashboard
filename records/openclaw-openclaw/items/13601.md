Keep the existing SQLite-backed cron history, then have maintainers narrow the next core increment: timestamp filters through run-log/protocol/UI/CLI, a deliberate `cron logs` or `cron history` CLI shape, aggregate reporting, export/follow semantics, and an explicit retention/pruning policy.
