## What This Changes

This PR changes QA Lab’s OpenClaw/Codex runtime-tool parity and coverage reports so required coverage requires a successful linked tool result, while report-only or unavailable fixtures preserve skip outcomes and no longer count as required coverage.

## System Context

QA Lab executes equivalent runtime-tool scenarios through OpenClaw and Codex, captures persisted call/result evidence, and compares the two cells. Its parity and coverage reports feed release-validation confidence rather than changing end-user runtime behavior.
