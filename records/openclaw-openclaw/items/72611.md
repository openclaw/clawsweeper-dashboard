## Summary

Keep open: current main has substantial archive and cron-classification protections, but no supported selective Dreaming exclusion policy; the remaining operator-facing configuration requires maintainer product direction and is not owned by the narrower open cron-promotion fix.

## What This Changes

Add reliable Dreaming exclusion for cron-derived and archived transcripts plus configurable filters for sessions, cron jobs, agents, or source patterns.

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt one typed memory-core ingestion policy that first fixes provenance and archive classification automatically, then offers a small supported control for disabling session ingestion or excluding well-defined source classes; avoid filesystem-path regexes as the primary contract and prove fresh-install and upgrade behavior.
