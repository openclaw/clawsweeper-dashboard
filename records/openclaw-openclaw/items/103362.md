## Summary

## What This Changes

Fix the iOS Share Extension so oversized images are delivered within its attachment budget or explicitly reported as omitted instead of silently succeeding.

## Best Possible Solution

Reuse or minimally extend the shared OpenClawKit JPEG transcoder for a Share Extension-specific byte and dimension budget, retain explicit omission state, and show an honest partial-send or failure status when an intended image cannot be included.
