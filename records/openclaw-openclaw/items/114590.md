## What This Changes

The PR makes Telegram reopen and update its progress draft when a reply enters the queued-followup lane, then removes that presentation state after final delivery or a failed attempt settles.

## System Context

Telegram dispatch owns draft and progress presentation while the shared auto-reply follow-up runner admits, executes, delivers, and completes queued turns. This change passes queued-turn admission and settlement signals from the runner to Telegram so users see progress between queue admission and final reply delivery.
