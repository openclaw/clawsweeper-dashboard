## What This Changes

Repair cron upgrades so legacy scheduled jobs remain present and controllable after the JSON-to-SQLite transition, while channel-less isolated jobs do not fail solely from implicit delivery.

## System Context

Cron jobs enter through the CLI or cron tool, are persisted in the shared state store, loaded by the gateway scheduler, and may perform optional channel delivery after an agent turn. Migration and delivery-default mistakes can therefore affect both durable schedule state and the status reported for completed work.
