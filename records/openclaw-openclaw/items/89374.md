## What This Changes

Issue reports that timeout compaction can log success while reusing an unrecoverable Codex-backed channel session, causing repeated timeouts or failed delivery until the session-store entry is reset.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a maintainer-approved Codex channel-session recovery policy that either proves post-compaction headroom before retrying or clearly rolls over/requires reset after repeated timeout compactions, with focused runner coverage and live high-context channel proof.
