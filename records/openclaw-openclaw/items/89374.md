## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 fix the narrow Codex automatic-compaction ownership skip, but the timeout-compaction path still retries after reported success and can fall through after repeated timeouts without proving safe headroom or rolling over the affected channel session.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to recover or roll over Codex-backed channel sessions when timeout compaction reports success but the same session remains stuck or unrecoverable.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a bounded in-product recovery policy for unrecoverable Codex channel sessions after repeated timeout compactions, such as automatic rollover with side-effect guards or a visible Doctor/Fix recovery action, then prove it with focused runner tests and live channel evidence.
