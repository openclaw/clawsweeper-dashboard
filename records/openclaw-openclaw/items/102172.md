## Best Possible Solution

Define a maintainer-approved claude-cli lifecycle contract that preserves process-tree cleanup safety while preventing turns from looking cleanly finished when CLI-owned background work is still alive, or at least surfaces typed telemetry/failure before forced cleanup.
