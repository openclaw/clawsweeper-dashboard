## Summary

The draft should stay open but is not merge-ready: its executor/background ownership split is coherent and all current supervisor callers are classified, yet abort still cannot reach turn-owned descendants that leave the executor's POSIX process group, which is the central safety boundary in the linked incident. The contributor now explicitly acknowledges this limitation and has kept the PR draft pending containment design and real service-managed proof.

## What This Changes

The PR makes supervised processes declare executor or background ownership, gives POSIX CLI executors isolated process groups for cancellation, preserves persistent background lifetimes, and adds focused tests and CLI-backend documentation.

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt one canonical turn-owned containment mechanism that can terminate autonomous descendants across process-group or session changes, preserve explicitly background-owned exec and cron work, and prove both abort and non-abort behavior on current-head service-managed gateways.
