## Summary

Keep open: the two prior blockers are fixed and the exact-head proof is strong, but the branch still has a merge-blocking process-identity defect; its fail-closed containment contract also remains an explicit maintainer choice.

## What This Changes

The PR makes ACPX cancellation close cached runtimes, drain detached lease-owned descendants, fail startup or abort when containment cannot be verified, and report containment failure instead of normal abort success.

Re-inventory each target and revalidate the same lease and Gateway identity immediately before every escalation signal, then retain the exact-head service proof and obtain explicit owner acceptance for fail-closed startup and runtime-handle eviction.
