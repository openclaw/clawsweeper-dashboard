## Summary

## Best Possible Solution

Apply a cleared deadline at the complete dispatcher `beforeDeliver` boundary, preserve owner-declared hook budgets, surface timeout as an observable delivery failure, and prove lane recovery with deterministic tests plus exact-head real output.
