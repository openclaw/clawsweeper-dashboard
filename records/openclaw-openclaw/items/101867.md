Add a maintainer-approved trusted subagent terminal diagnostic event and OTel mapping with stable kind/reason fields, redacted identifiers, and registry/exporter regression tests.

- **Defer to lifecycle redesign:** Fold this into broader lifecycle/audit work and accept that downstream subagent-death clustering remains blind until that design lands.
