## Summary

Keep open: current main and the latest release still derive the macOS Launch at login toggle from live LaunchAgent state, with no durable in-app intent or post-Sparkle-update reconciliation for the reported drift.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to keep the macOS app Launch at login setting enabled and honored across app restarts and Sparkle app updates.

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt one durable source of truth for the user’s Launch at login intent, reconcile the app LaunchAgent after signed Sparkle updates, and prove the behavior with a packaged macOS update plus login-cycle check.
