The issue asks for the macOS app Launch at login setting to remain enabled and continue auto-launching across app restarts and Sparkle updates.

## Best Possible Solution

Define the macOS app Launch at login source of truth, then add guarded app-owned intent persistence and startup or Sparkle-update reconciliation that restores only OpenClaw-owned LaunchAgent drift.
