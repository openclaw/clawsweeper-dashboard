Keep this open for a maintainer-sponsored memory-lancedb design that adds plugin-local FTS indexing, query-text propagation, explicit hybrid config/default policy, vector fallback, docs, and focused tests, or close only after maintainers explicitly choose external-only/vector-only behavior.
