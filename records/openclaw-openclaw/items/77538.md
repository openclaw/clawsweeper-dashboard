## Review Findings

Overall correctness: patch is incorrect

Overall confidence: 0.88

Full review comments:

- **[P1] Bound auth payload bytes, not grapheme count:** `packages/gateway-protocol/src/schema/primitives.ts:32-34`
  - body: This still does not bound the work the PR is trying to limit. TypeBox 1.3.3 evaluates `maxLength` as grapheme clusters, while `safeEqualSecret` allocates and pads UTF-8 bytes; a payload such as one base character plus many combining marks can stay under the 256-grapheme cap while still carrying tens of KB into the bootstrap/device verifier. Please enforce a byte limit or a token-shape/ASCII contract before these values reach secret comparison.
  - confidence: 0.9
- **[P1] Preserve or approve shared-secret compatibility:** `packages/gateway-protocol/src/schema/frames.ts:74-79`
  - body: Applying `HandshakeSharedSecretString` to `auth.token`, `auth.password`, and the runtime-token fields adds a new 4096-length protocol rejection even though `gateway.auth.token` and `gateway.auth.password` config currently accept unbounded secret strings. Existing outlier deployments with longer credentials can stop connecting after upgrade unless this cap is explicitly accepted, documented, and covered by upgrade/error proof, or the shared-secret fields keep the previous effective contract.
  - confidence: 0.84
