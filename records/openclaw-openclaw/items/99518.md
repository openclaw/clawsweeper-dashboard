## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Review and land or explicitly reject https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99530, aiming for one internal lifecycle classifier shared before durable allowlist or auto-review on gateway-host, node-host, and direct system.run while leaving read-only status/help paths cheap.
