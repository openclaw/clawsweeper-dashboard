- **[P1] Do not approve pending plans when leaving plan mode:** `ui/src/pages/chat/components/chat-controls.ts:220`
  - body: The mode selector sends `/plan exit` whenever switching away from plan, but `/plan exit` resolves a `pending_approval` plan with `PLAN_APPROVE_LABEL`.
