Full review comments:

- **[P1] Preserve the root-safe file-open boundary:** `src/gateway/session-utils.ts:188-201`
  - body: Use the existing `openRootFileSync` projection instead of validating a pathname and later reading it by pathname.   - confidence: 0.97
