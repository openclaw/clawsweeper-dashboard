## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical outbound Telegram album tracker and have maintainers review, port, or supersede the linked draft with a current-main Telegram plugin design covering message actions, reply delivery, outbound payload delivery, focused tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.
