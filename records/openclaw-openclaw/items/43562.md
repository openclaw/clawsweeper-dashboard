The issue asks for a configurable or neutral main-agent identity preamble so SOUL.md-defined personas are not overridden by the hardcoded opening line.

Settle the prompt-identity contract, then land or replace the open candidate with a refreshed narrow opt-in implementation that preserves defaults and includes docs/schema metadata, prompt/cache tests, and upgrade-safe behavior.
