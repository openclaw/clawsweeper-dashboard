Keep the fixed inbound debounce baseline, route adaptive timing to https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/57123, route Telegram bundle metadata to https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/87002, and decide semantic topic splitting as an opt-in core/plugin API design before implementation.
