## What This Changes

Prevent malformed Anthropic thinking signatures from being persisted or replayed, recover existing poisoned sessions, and avoid retrying deterministic signature-validation failures.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve opaque provider signatures byte-for-byte, identify and remove the U+2026 mutation path, reject or quarantine malformed signatures before provider replay, and add a bounded Doctor/session repair for already-poisoned transcripts while coordinating with the completed-turn replay policy in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99383.
