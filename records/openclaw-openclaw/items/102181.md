Current StepFun setup explicitly separates China and Global endpoints, so CN Step Plan video describe would send the request to the wrong host. Map `.com/step_plan/v1` to `.com/v1` and `.ai/step_plan/v1` to `.ai/v1`, or reject unsupported plan endpoints.
