## Best Possible Solution

## Reproduction Assessment

## Solution Assessment

No, not as-is: the env gate is a plausible product direction but not the best fix without maintainer-approved compatibility, migration, and docs/contract alignment; preserving default registration is safer unless product chooses opt-in.
