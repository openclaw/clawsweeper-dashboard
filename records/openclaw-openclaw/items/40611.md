## What This Changes

The issue reports that heartbeat skips caused by active requests retry too aggressively and can block Telegram or other channel conversations once the active turn releases the session lane.

Keep this issue as the canonical narrow bug until https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/89989 or an equivalent wake-layer retry fix merges; keep broader activity-based or non-blocking heartbeat redesign work separate.
