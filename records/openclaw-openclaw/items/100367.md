## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to prevent long message_tool_only turns from ending with withheld plain finals after the one-shot delivery reminder loses recency.

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt one maintainer-approved recovery design that either refreshes the tool-only delivery contract during long turns or retries a stranded final without auto-publishing private final text, then coordinate transcript-reality handling with https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/100368.
