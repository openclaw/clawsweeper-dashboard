## Summary

Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still allow cron jobs to default enabled, plugin scheduled turns still add enabled recurring agent-turn jobs without a payload model, and the remaining consent, provenance, model-cost, and quota circuit-breaker policy needs maintainer security/product direction.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to stop externally or plugin-registered recurring cron jobs from auto-enabling with inherited expensive models and to add approval, model-cost, dry-run, and quota circuit-breaker safeguards.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one cron safety policy for externally or plugin-registered recurring `agentTurn` jobs that covers operator-visible activation, non-forgeable provenance, explicit model or cost-warning behavior, doctor/update visibility, and quota-aware pause or notification semantics.
