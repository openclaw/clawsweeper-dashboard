## Summary

Keep open: current main still intentionally keeps DuckDuckGo and other key-free managed web_search providers explicit-only, and the open candidate PR that would reverse that policy has not merged.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to make keyless DuckDuckGo web_search work without an explicit `tools.web.search.provider` selection, or at least warn/configure it when enabled but unselected.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep key-free managed web_search explicit unless maintainers deliberately approve a full policy reversal; otherwise solve the UX gap through diagnostics/setup guidance and close or narrow the linked candidate PR.
