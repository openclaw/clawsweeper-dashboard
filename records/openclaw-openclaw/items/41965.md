## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 support per-call `forceDocument`/`asDocument` for Telegram and WhatsApp, but the requested durable `MEDIA:#doc` syntax or deployer-level channel default is still absent and needs a maintainer product/API decision.
