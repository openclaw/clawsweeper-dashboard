## Summary

Keep open: the requested group admission workflow remains a product and security design decision, and the merged DM pairing hook plus adjacent unknown-group proposals do not establish pending group approval, owner-authorized approval/rejection, or a safe pre-approval observation contract.

## What This Changes

Add a cross-channel group-pairing flow that notifies an owner, records pending groups, supports approval or rejection, and optionally permits restricted observation before approval.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one additive, fail-closed cross-channel group-admission contract with persisted channel/account/group state, owner-visible join metadata, authorized approve/reject operations, and explicit no-content-before-approval defaults; scope reader-agent observation separately unless maintainers deliberately approve it as part of that contract.
