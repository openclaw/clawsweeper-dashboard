## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still keep pairing scoped to DMs while group admission remains open, allowlist, or disabled. The related Telegram and WhatsApp items overlap on unknown-group safety, but they do not replace the requested cross-channel owner-notified approval flow or pending read-only mode.

## What This Changes

The issue requests a cross-channel group pairing flow with owner notifications, approve/reject operations, and optional read-only pending-group handling.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a shared group-admission contract first: fail closed by default, store pending approvals by channel/account/group, send redacted owner notifications, authorize approve/reject operations, and scope reader-agent observation as a separate explicit mode.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes at source level, not as a bug: current main keeps group policies to open, allowlist, or disabled and keeps pairing on the DM path only.

## Solution Assessment

Unclear until maintainers decide the product/security semantics; the maintainable path is a shared group-admission contract rather than stretching DM pairing or merging narrower channel-specific proposals independently.
