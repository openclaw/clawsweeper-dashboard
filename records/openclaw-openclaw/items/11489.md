## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still keep pairing on the DM path and group access on open, allowlist, or disabled behavior; the related items cover narrower Telegram/WhatsApp fail-closed or listen-only behavior and do not replace the requested owner-notified cross-channel group approval flow.

## What This Changes

The issue requests group pairing for unconfigured Telegram/WhatsApp groups, with owner notification, approve/reject controls, and optional read-only pending-group routing.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a product/security-reviewed group admission contract that stores pending approvals by channel, account, and group, sends redacted owner notifications, authorizes approve/reject operations, and treats read-only observation as a separate explicit isolated mode or plugin path.
