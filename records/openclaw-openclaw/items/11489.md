## Label Justifications

- `impact:security`: The issue is about approving or rejecting untrusted groups before the bot engages and limiting prompt-injection exposure from shared rooms.

## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still keep pairing scoped to DMs, while group admission remains open, disabled, or allowlist only. The related Telegram and WhatsApp items are narrower mitigations and do not implement owner-notified group approval, pending group state, or a cross-channel read-only pending-group contract.

## What This Changes

The issue requests group pairing for bot-added groups, owner notification, approve/reject controls, and optional read-only pending-group observation.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a product/security-reviewed group admission contract that stores pending approvals by channel/account/group, sends redacted owner notifications, authorizes approve/reject operations, and treats read-only observation as a separate explicit isolated mode unless maintainers fold it into the same contract.
