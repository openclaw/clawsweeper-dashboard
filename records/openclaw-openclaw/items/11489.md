## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still scope pairing to DMs, while group admission remains allowlist/open/disabled. Related Telegram, WhatsApp, and listen-only work covers narrower warn/leave or observation paths, but not this broader owner-notified group approval contract.

## What This Changes

Request group pairing for unconfigured Telegram/WhatsApp-style groups, with owner notification, approve/reject controls, and optional read-only pending-group handling.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a product/security-reviewed group admission contract that stores pending approvals by channel/account/group, sends redacted owner notifications, authorizes approve/reject operations, and treats read-only observation as a separate explicit isolated mode.
