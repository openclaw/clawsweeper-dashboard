## Best Possible Solution

Define a maintainer-approved web_fetch SSRF policy surface, likely under explicit dangerous ssrfPolicy naming, with hard metadata/link-local behavior, redirect/cache/provider/audit coverage, and per-agent or requester authorization before landing the config change.
