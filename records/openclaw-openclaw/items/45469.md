The issue asks for GatewayClient.scheduleReconnect() to stop retrying forever by adding a bounded reconnect policy.

## Best Possible Solution

Land or replace the linked PR with a package-level opt-in reconnect cap, unchanged unlimited node-host recovery by default, maintainer-approved short-lived caller wiring, focused tests, and redacted real reconnect proof.
