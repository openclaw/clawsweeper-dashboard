## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still use synchronous Signal daemon shutdown, and the focused closing fix remains open at https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/71863.

## What This Changes

The issue reports that Signal hot-reload/restart sends SIGTERM to signal-cli without waiting for exit, leaving orphaned processes and causing send failures.

## Best Possible Solution

Land https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/71863 or an equivalent Signal-owned lifecycle fix that makes daemon stop async, idempotent, bounded by SIGKILL fallback, and awaited through monitor teardown.
