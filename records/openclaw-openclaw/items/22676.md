## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still use fire-and-forget Signal daemon shutdown, and the focused closing fix remains open at https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/71863.

## What This Changes

The issue reports that Signal auto-start daemon shutdown during SIGUSR1/config restarts sends SIGTERM without awaiting exit, causing orphaned signal-cli processes and failed sends.

## Best Possible Solution

Land https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/71863, or an equivalent focused Signal-owned fix that awaits daemon exit after SIGTERM, escalates after a bounded timeout, and keeps monitor teardown open until cleanup finishes.
