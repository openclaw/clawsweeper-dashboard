## What This Changes

The branch replaces Discord’s replay guard with a durable ingress drain that persists raw `MESSAGE_CREATE` payloads before dispatch and settles them through turn adoption.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve durable-before-dispatch admission while allowing messages eligible for the same Discord debounce batch to reach the debouncer together, and propagate the actual deferred-flush error into the drain so retryability and dead-letter policy remain canonical.
