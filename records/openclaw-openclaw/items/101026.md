- `P1`: The issue reports a source-reproducible silent outbound hook bypass affecting real Feishu group-chat plugin workflows and safety/moderation hooks.

The issue asks for Feishu auto-reply, durable message-tool sends, and normal inbound channel handling to fire outbound and claim hooks consistently across delivery paths.

## Best Possible Solution
