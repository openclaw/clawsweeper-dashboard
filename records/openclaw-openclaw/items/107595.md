The branch makes fallback approval callbacks in QQBot direct C2C conversations bypass command authorization and adds tests for absent and wildcard DM allowlists.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve explicit approval authorization; either use the existing concrete allowFrom/approver identity contract, or introduce a maintainer-approved and verifiable QQ owner-binding mechanism with owner and untrusted-open-DM regression coverage.
