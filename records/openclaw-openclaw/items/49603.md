## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 include a partial self-owned lock cleanup, but the startup sweep still preserves current-PID locks without starttime and the remaining live same-PID recovery policy is not safe to cleanup-close.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to clear orphaned `agents/*/sessions/*.jsonl.lock` files owned by the starting Gateway PID so sessions do not wedge after restart or lost in-memory lock state.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this issue open until maintainers either land a focused startup-cleanup policy for legacy/current-PID locks or explicitly fold the remaining live self-lock recovery into the run-lifecycle reliability tracker.
