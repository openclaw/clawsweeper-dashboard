## Summary

## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the plugin-owned shared WebSocket option builder, but merge only after a Codex owner confirms the 30-second policy for both transports and the intended error/exit contract; otherwise calibrate the transports separately or narrow the patch to the timeout behavior.
