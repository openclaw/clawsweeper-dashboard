Split this into narrower work: a focused relevance- or structure-aware bootstrap selector, compaction work tracked through the existing provider/context-engine surfaces and https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/58398, and observation extraction kept plugin-driven unless a concrete SDK/API gap is proven.
