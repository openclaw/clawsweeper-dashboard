## What This Changes

The PR disables Telegram answer and reasoning previews whenever rewrite- or cancellation-capable outbound hooks are registered, while retaining previews for hook-free and observer-only configurations.

## System Context

Telegram can display provider-side draft and progress previews before OpenClaw’s ordinary outbound delivery pipeline completes. This change decides whether those early previews may be emitted, then either streams them or defers visible output to the normal final-message delivery path.
