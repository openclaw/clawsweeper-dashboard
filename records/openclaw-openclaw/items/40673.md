Define one documented sensitive-logging precedence contract, preferably making explicit disable intent win over stale persisted service env, then add LaunchAgent/service-env tests, docs, and doctor/status guidance.

## Solution Assessment

Unclear until maintainers choose the precedence contract. The narrow durable fix should settle that contract across LaunchAgent env files, state-dir `.env`, config env, docs, and doctor/status checks.
