## Label Justifications

- `P2`: The shared channel give-up boundary is broken, but restart volume remains bounded by manager backoff and monitor throttling rather than becoming a tight process-level loop.
- `impact:crash-loop`: The monitor can clear consecutive-failure progress and repeatedly revive a permanently failing channel account.
- `maturity:stable`: The affected Gateway lifecycle and health-monitor behavior belongs to the M4 Stable gateway-runtime feature.

## Summary

Keep open: current main still allows the default health monitor to erase manager-owned retry progress and restart accounts after the manager’s give-up threshold, and no related issue or PR owns this exact defect.

## What This Changes

Prevent the channel health monitor from clearing in-progress crash-loop accounting or reviving a channel account after the manager gives up.

## Best Possible Solution

Put recovery ownership behind a manager-level operation or closed recovery-state result that distinguishes active backoff, terminal give-up, and externally unhealthy or wedged tasks, then cover all three states with a real manager-plus-monitor integration regression.

Yes, at high confidence from current-main source: a real manager startup failure enters positive-attempt backoff, while the real monitor excludes that state from continuing recovery and deletes its counter before a task-guarded start. ## Solution Assessment

Yes in direction, but the narrowest maintainable solution is a manager-owned recovery contract rather than another monitor-side heuristic over `running`, `restartPending`, and `reconnectAttempts`.
