## Summary

This remains a useful hardening PR, but the current head still converts a readable oversized ignore policy into no policy at all, so previously excluded workspace content can be traversed and loaded. The prior P1 finding remains applicable because the reviewed head SHA has not changed.

## What This Changes

The PR caps reads of `.gitignore`, `.ignore`, and `.fdignore` files at 4 MiB, preserves symlink-chain resolution, and adds focused regression tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the bounded filesystem read, but make an oversized readable policy fail closed for its scan subtree or preserve its exclusions through a bounded parsing design, with regression coverage proving excluded content is never admitted.

## Solution Assessment

No. Bounding the read through the shared filesystem wrapper is appropriate, but silently discarding the entire valid policy is not the safest or most compatible solution; oversized handling should fail closed or retain the exclusions with bounded memory.
