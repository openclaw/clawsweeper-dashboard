## Summary

Keep open. Current main still leaves direct inbound Feishu interactive-card messages on the generic raw-content path, while the merged and open related work only covers fetched/quoted cards, card-action callbacks, outbound card sends, or merge_forward subsets.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to parse Feishu interactive card message content, including card text, buttons, dropdown options, and form values, instead of exposing only placeholders or raw type/content identifiers.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the broad Feishu interactive-card parsing tracker, wire direct inbound cards to the existing parser, and resolve callback-field and merge_forward payload details through their narrower linked items after maintainers choose the stable agent-visible shape.
