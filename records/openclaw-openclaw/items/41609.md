## Summary

Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still leave direct inbound Feishu `interactive` messages on the raw-content path, while merged related work only covers fetched or quoted cards and narrower open items cover callback fields and merge_forward variants.

## What This Changes

The issue asks for Feishu interactive-card messages to expose readable card text and useful card/action fields to agents instead of placeholders or raw payloads.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the broad Feishu interactive-card parsing tracker, reuse the existing Feishu card parser for direct inbound `interactive` messages, and resolve callback-field plus merge_forward payload details through the narrower linked items after maintainers choose a stable contract.
