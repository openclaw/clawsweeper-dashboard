## Summary

Keep open. The parent-scoped design is promising, but the current head appears to reset its publish quota whenever an iframe reload recreates the bridge, and the supplied validation is tests and CI rather than real browser behavior; the permanent untrusted-widget messaging contract also needs explicit owner approval.

## What This Changes

Adds a capability-gated, parent-owned, tab-scoped publish/subscribe bus for approved Workspaces custom widgets, including lifecycle cleanup, payload and rate limits, tests, and documentation.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the publish quota in the parent-owned widget connection so it survives iframe reloads, cover that lifecycle with a regression test, then require redacted real-browser proof and explicit Workspaces-owner approval before merging the new capability contract.
