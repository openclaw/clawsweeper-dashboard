## Summary

Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still keep enforceable before/after tool-call policy in typed plugin and session-extension paths, while managed HOOK.md internal hooks remain void-returning command/lifecycle handlers without a tool-call event or blocking/parameter-rewrite contract.

## What This Changes

Expose the existing before_tool_call and after_tool_call capabilities through managed/internal HOOK.md hooks, including pre-tool blocking or rewrites and after-call observation.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical design tracker until maintainers either add a typed managed HOOK.md tool-call contract with block/rewrite/observe semantics, validation, docs, and tests, or explicitly keep tool-call policy plugin-only and surface unsupported managed hook event names.

- **Telemetry-only internal events:** Expose after-call or non-blocking tool observation to managed hooks while keeping pre-tool blocking and parameter rewrites in the typed plugin contract.
