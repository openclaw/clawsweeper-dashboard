## What This Changes

The issue requests a durable cross-channel contract requiring sanitized final fallback text to be delivered or reported as a real delivery failure, with explicit sent/failed/partial/suppressed outcomes and recovery visibility.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical maintainer-owned delivery contract until the remaining channel slices are landed or replaced, then add shared SDK/core conformance and diagnostics coverage across durable text-capable channels.
