## Summary

The PR should stay open because request-driven collection still permits an unattended meeting to overflow the 50-line browser buffer and silently lose most of its transcript. The current head also fails lint, lacks live Google Meet proof, and needs feature-owner acceptance of its volatile restart and eviction semantics.

## What This Changes

Adds bounded in-memory Google Meet caption transcript accumulation with cursor-based retrieval through a gateway method, tool action, CLI command, tests, and documentation.

## Best Possible Solution

Use one lifecycle-owned, non-duplicating caption drain per active transcribe session, stop it on every leave/end path, preserve bounded in-memory cursor semantics, and document volatile retention only after feature-owner acceptance and live proof beyond 50 unattended captions.
