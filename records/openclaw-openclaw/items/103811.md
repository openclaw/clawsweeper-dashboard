## Summary

This PR remains useful and directly addresses the linked feature issue, but the new background drain is broader and longer-lived than the caption lifecycle it serves, real Google Meet behavior is still unproven, and the bounded volatile transcript contract needs feature-owner approval.

## What This Changes

Adds bounded in-memory Google Meet caption transcript accumulation with automatic draining and cursor-based retrieval through a gateway method, tool action, CLI command, tests, and documentation.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep gateway-side accumulation in the Google Meet plugin, but restrict draining to managed Chrome transcribe sessions, retire it through the canonical end path when Chrome health shows the meeting ended, and land only after redacted live proof plus owner approval of the documented volatile retention contract.
