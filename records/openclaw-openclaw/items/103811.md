## Best Possible Solution

Keep the implementation plugin-local, enforce non-negative integer cursors consistently at every gateway, tool, CLI, and runtime boundary, demonstrate live caption accumulation and incremental retrieval, and land only after the Google Meet owner accepts the documented restart and eviction semantics.
