## Label Justifications

- `P1`: A deterministic, shipped Google Meet workflow reports successful departure while the participant remains active in the meeting.
- `impact:session-state`: OpenClaw marks the session ended even though the corresponding browser participant remains connected and receiving meeting content.
- `impact:ux-friction`: Operators must manually close the tab or maintain separate browser automation after the CLI claims success.

## Summary

Keep open: both v2026.6.11 and current main report the session ended without performing a Chrome browser departure, and the linked candidate fix remains open and lacks live after-fix Google Meet proof.

## What This Changes

Make Google Meet Chrome-session leave actually remove the browser participant, or accurately describe a tracking-only operation.

## Best Possible Solution

Persist the exact browser target and tab ownership on each session, separate internal bridge reassignment from user-requested leave, use a locale-robust graceful departure control, close only plugin-owned tabs when necessary, and verify the result in a live meeting.
