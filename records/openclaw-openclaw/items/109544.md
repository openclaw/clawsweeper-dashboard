The latest head also lacks after-fix real-flow proof and requires approval-policy owner direction.

## Best Possible Solution

Validate required skill metadata and a non-empty body at the service boundary, auto-apply only creates or updates that preserve every existing substantive instruction line, require explicit approval for replacements, show the bounded change summary before writing, and prove the exact head through the real approval flow.
