## Summary

The resource-exhaustion hardening is valid, narrowly placed, and backed by convincing real TCP proof, but the new fail-closed behavior can take an established IRC account offline and the review does not prove that the gateway reconnects after this socket closure; an IRC owner should confirm the recovery policy before merge.

## What This Changes

Adds UTF-8 byte-length enforcement for complete and pending inbound IRC records, closes the socket on a 512-byte limit violation, and adds real TCP boundary and rejection coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep byte-bounded parsing at the IRC client boundary, but pair fail-closed disconnection with an owner-approved, backoff-controlled gateway recovery path and an end-to-end test proving the account returns to service after an oversized record.
