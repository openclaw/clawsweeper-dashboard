## Summary

Keep open for maintainer review: the client-layer guard is technically sound and well proven, but merging it makes one oversized server record terminate an established IRC account without proving or selecting the channel recovery behavior.

## What This Changes

Adds a 512-byte UTF-8 inbound IRC record limit for complete and pending lines, closes the socket on violation, and adds real TCP boundary, fragmentation, and multibyte regression coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the strict bounded parser guard, then either make the IRC gateway observe post-ready client termination and recover with the channel’s normal backoff lifecycle, or explicitly document and test an intentional fail-closed operator-restart contract.
