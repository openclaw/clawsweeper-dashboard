## Summary

Keep this PR open for maintainer review: the resource-exhaustion bug is real, but the proposed fail-closed policy can strand a registered IRC account offline until the account or gateway is restarted because the passive monitor does not observe or recover from post-ready socket closure.

## What This Changes

The PR enforces the IRC inbound byte limit on complete and pending records, closes the socket on violations, and adds real TCP boundary and rejection coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Bound input at the IRC framing owner and make recovery explicit: preferably discard the oversized record with bounded state until its delimiter, or retain disconnect semantics only after wiring socket closure into supervised reconnect and backoff.
