- **Fence wake generations (recommended):** Capture the owning session generation and skip, supersede, or leave inspectable any wake whose session was reset before delivery.

- **Defer durable handoff:** Keep wake inspection only and postpone automatic session-queue handoff until a broader durable-session lifecycle contract is approved.
