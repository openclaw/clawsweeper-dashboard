Question: Should a resolved durable wake remain bound to the session generation that owned it, or may it be delivered to a new generation sharing the same logical session key after reset?

Rationale: Both behaviors are technically implementable, but they encode different session-state and stale-context policies that existing queue mechanics cannot choose automatically.

Likely owner: fuller-stack-dev

Owner confidence: medium

Options:

- **Bind to session generation (recommended):** Record the resolved session ID and leave the wake pending or inspectable when that generation no longer matches, preventing stale wake context from entering a reset session.
- **Follow the logical key:** Explicitly permit delivery into a replacement session generation and document and test that resets do not discard outstanding durable obligations.
- **Defer automatic handoff:** Keep durable wakes inspection-only until the reset-ownership policy is settled.
