TypeScript does not preserve that optional-property narrowing into the closure, and the current `check-prod-types` job fails with TS2322 here. Capture `const factory = runner.factory` before returning the registration, or otherwise make the callback close over a non-optional factory.

  - body: The registry currently returns `{ adapter }` where `adapter` is `QaTransportAdapter | QaHostedTransportAdapter`, which is assignable to neither member of the declared result union and also leaks into `createQaTransportAdapter`. Split the hosted and flow result branches or otherwise discriminate the result before returning so `check-prod-types` can pass.
  - confidence: 0.97
