This PR addresses a real credential-file symlink hazard and includes strong terminal proof, but its shared `replace-file` wrapper now duplicates a current-main upstream delegation at a security-sensitive boundary. Keep it open for a targeted rebase and an explicit owner decision on whether the permanent no-follow/mode contract belongs in OpenClaw or `@openclaw/fs-safe`.

The branch routes missing `auth.json` creation through atomic replacement and adds OpenClaw wrappers around async and sync `@openclaw/fs-safe` replacements to avoid final-path chmod symlink races while preserving modes.

## Best Possible Solution
