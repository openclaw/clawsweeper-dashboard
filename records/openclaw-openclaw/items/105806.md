## Summary

Keep this PR open but do not merge it at the current head: the patch does not cover the phase-less phantom state reported by the linked issue, may reclaim a newly terminal operation before its own settlement window ends, and still lacks real lifecycle proof of safe lane recovery.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one recovery invariant that reclaims reply claims only when concrete run, handle, registry, and progress evidence proves they are phantom or settled, preserve operations still inside their own terminal-settlement window, and demonstrate the full retained-operation-to-lane-recovery lifecycle with redacted terminal output.
