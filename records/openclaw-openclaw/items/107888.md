## Summary

This PR restores the reported custom-identity preview path, but removing the guard also exposes identity-authored drafts to cleanup paths that call `chat.delete`; Slack does not permit deletion of messages posted with customized authorship, so media, error, or preview-finalization fallback can leave a stale preview beside the final reply.

## What This Changes

Removes the Slack custom-identity exclusion from portable draft-preview selection and updates dispatch and delivery-trace regression expectations.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep custom-identity portable previews, but make every terminal and fallback path finalize or safely replace the preview without relying on deletion, with regression coverage and live Slack proof for normal text plus separate-delivery/error cases.
