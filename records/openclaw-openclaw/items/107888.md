Keep open: the previously reported P1 blocker remains on the unchanged reviewed head. ([docs.slack.dev](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/methods/chat.delete/?utm_source=openai))

## What This Changes

The PR removes Slack’s custom-identity exclusion from portable draft initialization and updates regression expectations for identity-aware preview creation and in-place finalization.

## Best Possible Solution

Support custom-identity previews with a terminal-delivery design that never relies on deleting an impersonated Slack message, and prove successful final edits plus media, split-render, error, and edit-failure fallbacks leave exactly one coherent terminal answer.
