## Summary

The current head still has both previously reported P1 defects: retries re-enter the whole assembled turn, repeating session recording and post-record callbacks, while exhaustion rethrows a plain error that Discord’s queue commits rather than making replayable. The focused tests are useful, but the branch is not merge-safe until retry ownership and replay finalization are corrected; live Discord proof is also absent.

## What This Changes

Adds bounded exponential-backoff retries to Discord inbound reply dispatch for reply-session initialization conflicts, plus abort, exhaustion, unrelated-error, and history regression tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Prepare and record the Discord inbound turn once, retry only the dispatch/session-initialization operation, and route exhausted conflicts through Discord’s retryable replay-release contract; then validate this defense-in-depth behavior against the open shared self-heal with a live Discord conflict-to-success trace.
