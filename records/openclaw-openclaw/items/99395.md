The issue reports that a top-level session using `sessions_yield` after parallel subagent fan-out can park permanently after the last child settles instead of synthesizing a final answer.

## Best Possible Solution

Review and land https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99396, or an equivalent top-level requester-settle wake through the existing announce pipeline, with coverage for delivered completions, give-up or suspension, and concurrent last-child settle races.
