## What This Changes

This PR centralizes no-visible-reply fallback decisions in a per-turn settled-delivery ledger and treats source-routed message-tool replies as observed delivery to avoid silent or duplicate channel replies.

## System Context

The auto-reply pipeline turns agent output, hooks, and routed channel sends into outbound replies. Its finalization step decides whether an otherwise-visible turn needs the standard fallback and whether a channel-level recovery consumer may act.
