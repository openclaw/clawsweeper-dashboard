## Summary

Keep this PR open for a narrow repair and human review: the latest paired-node recovery path can reopen a Meet tab instead of recovering the tracked one, and the stricter unknown-microphone policy is an intentional compatibility decision that needs maintainer confirmation and live behavior proof.

## What This Changes

The branch prevents transcript cursor replay across retention gaps, prefers active meeting tabs over Google sign-in fallbacks, and requires an explicit unmuted microphone observation before Google Meet talkback starts.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve the tracked browser session during chrome-node bridge recovery by forcing recovery to use `chrome.launch: false`, then land the explicit-unmuted policy only with maintainer confirmation and redacted live Meet or paired-node evidence for blocked, unmuted, and recovery states.
