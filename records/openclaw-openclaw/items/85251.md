## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to prevent Codex app-server and shared embedded-run channel turns from wedging silently after accepted work, and to surface or recover a retryable channel-visible outcome instead of losing replies.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one maintainer-approved session/harness lifecycle contract that treats Codex sidecar bindings as provider-owned for implicit reset, serializes rollover with listener registration, preserves recoverable completed output, and emits one visible retryable channel outcome when recovery cannot safely deliver.
