## What This Changes

The PR caps trailing blank-line appends at `maxLineLength - 1` and adds table-driven regression coverage in the auto-reply chunker.

## Best Possible Solution

Cap trailing blank lines by the remaining capacity of the final chunk, preserve ordinary trailing blanks when they fit, and add regression cases asserting every returned chunk stays within the limit.
