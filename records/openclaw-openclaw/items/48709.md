## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 fixed the Gemini tagged/native reasoning conflict, but they still persist and replay valid same-route Gemini visible textSignature values, so the central session-bloat and downstream message-loss risk remains unresolved.

## What This Changes

Requests fixing Gemini 2.5 Pro session failures caused by unbounded textSignature replay, embedded thinking tags, and mixed text/tool response delivery.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a Google provider replay policy that preserves required tool-call and native-thinking signatures while bounding, externalizing, or dropping optional visible-text textSignature values; keep broader inter-tool channel delivery policy on https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/25592.
