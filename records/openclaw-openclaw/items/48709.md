## Summary

Keep open: current main has fixed important adjacent Gemini tagged-reasoning behavior, but it still stores same-route Google text-part signatures and replays them as Gemini `thoughtSignature`, so the central session-bloat/replay contract remains unresolved.

## What This Changes

The issue reports Gemini session bloat and lost Telegram replies from large text signatures, tagged thinking text, and mixed text plus tool-call responses.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a bounded Google replay policy that preserves required Gemini native-thinking and tool-call signatures while capping, detaching, pruning, or otherwise preventing visible-text signatures from dominating session history and channel delivery.
