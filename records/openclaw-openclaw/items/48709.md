## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 fixed the tagged/native Gemini reasoning subset, but the Google transport still stores visible Gemini text signatures and replays them as thoughtSignature values, so the central session-bloat failure remains unresolved.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to stop Gemini 2.5 Pro textSignature growth, think-tag suppression, and mixed text/tool turns from causing bloated sessions and dropped Telegram replies.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a Google-provider replay policy that preserves required tool-call/native-thinking signatures while bounding, externalizing, or dropping optional visible-text signatures, then prove it with focused replay tests and a live Gemini tool-turn plus Telegram delivery check.
