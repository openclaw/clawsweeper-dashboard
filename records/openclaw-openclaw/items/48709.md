## Summary

Keep open: current main has fixed the tagged/native Gemini reasoning conflict and added malformed-signature guards, but valid same-route Gemini text/thought signatures are still retained and replayed, so the central session-bloat policy remains unresolved.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to stop Gemini 2.5 Pro session failures caused by retained text signatures, tagged reasoning text, and mixed text/tool turns that can bloat session history and suppress Telegram replies.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one bounded Gemini signature retention policy that preserves only provider-required replay continuity, keeps large opaque signature data out of normal session bloat where safe, and preserves the native-reasoning and empty-turn safeguards already on main.

## Maintainer Decision

Required: true

Kind: product_direction

Question: For same-route Google Gemini replay, should OpenClaw keep valid Gemini text/thought signatures in persisted session history, strip them from history, or move them into a bounded replay-owned store/metadata shape?

Rationale: Google's current contract makes signatures relevant for multi-turn reasoning continuity, while this issue shows that retaining them verbatim can bloat session history and break compaction/delivery, so automation should not choose the policy alone.

Likely owner: wAngByg

Owner reason: Their recent merged transport validation work is the closest history signal for the remaining Gemini signature replay contract.

Owner confidence: medium

Options:

- **Bound retained signatures (recommended):** Keep only the minimum required same-route Gemini signatures in a bounded canonical replay store or compact metadata shape and strip large opaque values from ordinary session history.
- **Strip signatures from history:** Drop Gemini text/thought signatures during persistence or replay to eliminate bloat, accepting possible loss of provider reasoning/tool-call continuity.
- **Keep validation-only behavior:** Treat malformed-signature validation and native reasoning as sufficient and accept valid signature growth as a provider cost.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes for source-level reproduction of the remaining risk: current main still carries valid Gemini text/thought signatures through same-route replay paths. I did not run a live Gemini 2.5 Pro multi-turn Telegram session, so the full timeout/message-loss chain is not live-reproduced here.

## Solution Assessment

Unclear until maintainers choose the signature contract. The native-reasoning and malformed-signature fixes are good partial solutions, but the best remaining fix needs a bounded retention or migration policy rather than another ad hoc strip/forward rule.
