## What This Changes

The branch makes POSIX `command`, `builtin`, and `exec` argv carriers resolve to their carried executable for exec allowlist evaluation, while blocking ambiguous forms and `command -p`.

## Best Possible Solution

Retain the narrow POSIX-only unwrapping with fail-closed ambiguity handling, then land it only after the exec-approval policy owner confirms that the carried executable—not the carrier—is the intended persistent allowlist identity.
