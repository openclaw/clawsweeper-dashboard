## Summary

Add a default-off Discord guild/channel option to silently transcribe unmentioned audio in requireMention channels and retain the transcript for later mentioned history.

Define a default-off Discord-owned silent transcription history policy with explicit consent, retention, cost, inheritance, schema, docs, and focused requireMention history tests.
