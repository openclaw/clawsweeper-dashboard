## What This Changes

The PR adds a current-session-scoped deferred `resetSession(reason?)` capability to successful, resettable after-compaction hook paths and drains requests after their owning embedded lifecycle.

Ship an owner-approved, hook-specific contract that exposes reset only after successful unlocked compaction, drains it after all active native and embedded work exits, documents deferred acceptance and failure semantics, preserves model-lock behavior, and has live proof of session rotation plus a successful next turn.
