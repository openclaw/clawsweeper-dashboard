Land a narrow after-compaction-only API after an owner chooses the permanent call shape, require every exposing lifecycle owner to reconcile the committed reset before later writes, split the critical-loop and unrelated CI/test changes, and prove one latest-head direct reply plus one unattended or cron path from reset request through a successful fresh-session turn.
