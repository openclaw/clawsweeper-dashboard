Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still do not provide the requested end-to-end channel creation path: Discord has partial provider/runtime support, but the CLI/docs expose only channel info/list, and Slack lacks channel-create discovery, dispatch/runtime support, scopes, and gates.
