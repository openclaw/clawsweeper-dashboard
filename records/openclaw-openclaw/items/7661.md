Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still do not provide the requested end-to-end channel-creation path: Discord has partial provider/runtime support but no shipped CLI/docs command, and Slack still lacks channel-create discovery, dispatch, scopes, and gates.

## Best Possible Solution
