## What This Changes

The branch adds recovery-point capture and restore plumbing that holds Gateway work admission until restored SQLite state, scheduler reconciliation, and owner readiness are durably confirmed, plus a read-only `gateway.restore.status` RPC.

## System Context

Gateway startup normally opens work admission after its runtime services are ready. This change feeds a host-supplied restored-recovery descriptor into startup, keeps admission closed while the restored scheduler and owners reconcile, and exposes the resulting held-or-ready state to authenticated operators.
