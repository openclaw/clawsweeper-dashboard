## Summary

Keep open: the patch appears technically sound and has convincing live proof, but merging it commits OpenClaw to a product-level definition of which signals make a yielded turn safe to leave silent; that continuation contract still needs explicit owner confirmation.

## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Confirm one canonical continuation-evidence contract, keep it centralized beside incomplete-turn resolution, land the additive warning with both positive and suppression tests, and handle any stricter tool-level rejection as separately approved follow-up work.
