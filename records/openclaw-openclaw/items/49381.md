## Summary

Keep open: current main still permits a failed/errored Feishu final closeout and a later recovered final to both become visible, while the direct Feishu candidate PR is closed unmerged and the broader durable-delivery issue does not fully own this duplicate-final failover case.

## What This Changes

Feishu direct-message turns can produce duplicate final replies when a rate-limited primary model fails over to a successful fallback model.

## Best Possible Solution

Land or adopt a focused Feishu/final-delivery coordination fix that treats failed-primary plus recovered-fallback output as one user-visible turn while preserving intentional distinct diagnostics, with regression tests and redacted live Feishu failover proof.

- **Fold Into Durable Delivery Roadmap:** Track this under the broader durable final-delivery contract and defer a Feishu-local fix until that cross-channel contract is designed.
