## Summary

Keep open: the protected `maintainer` label prevents cleanup close, and current `main` has per-gateway cache/outbox groundwork but not the requested saved-gateway registry, quick switcher, or full credential/device-integration migration.

## What This Changes

The issue requests first-class multi-gateway support in the iOS and Android apps: a paired-gateway registry, quick switching, per-gateway credentials, isolated state, and active-gateway device integrations.

## Best Possible Solution

Coordinate an iOS and Android design that adds a canonical paired-gateway registry keyed by stable identities, migrates single-slot credentials once, switches by tearing down/rebuilding active sessions, and proves cache, outbox, push, watch, and voice isolation across gateways.
