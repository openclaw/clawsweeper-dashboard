## What This Changes

The PR credits a successful requester wake separately from failed required visible delivery, persists both outcomes through cleanup and SQLite restore, and marks linked detached-task delivery failed.

## Best Possible Solution

Decouple requester-wake credit from visible-delivery accounting while retaining bounded pending/retry state for required visible output until it succeeds or exhausts an owner-approved policy, then persist and prove both outcomes across restart.
