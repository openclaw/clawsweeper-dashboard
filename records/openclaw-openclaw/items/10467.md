## What This Changes

Requests caller-selectable sub-agent queue lanes and compatible per-lane concurrency limits for `sessions_spawn`.

## Best Possible Solution

Expose only operator-declared sub-agent lane ids, keep omitted calls on `subagent`, preserve the existing scalar as the aggregate/default cap, and enforce a total budget so adding lanes cannot accidentally increase process-wide concurrency.

Options:

- **Declared lanes plus aggregate cap (recommended):** Allow only configured lane ids, preserve `subagent` as the default, and enforce both per-lane and total sub-agent limits.
- **Arbitrary dynamic lanes:** Accept caller-selected lane strings with defaults, gaining flexibility but increasing resource-bypass and configuration-drift risk.
- **Keep one global lane:** Retain the current pool and address fairness through per-agent caps or queue policy instead of new lane identities.
