## Summary

Keep open: current `main` still packs adjacent blank-line-separated paragraphs into one chunk before the configured block coalescer sees them, while the public streaming contract describes newline mode as splitting on paragraph boundaries and coalescing as the layer that merges streamed blocks.

**Likely related people**
- Tyler Yust — medium confidence; the behavior appears related to earlier paragraph-boundary block-streaming work in commit `9ef24fd400`.

## What This Changes

Ensure WhatsApp automatic replies preserve paragraph block boundaries until the configured block coalescer decides whether to merge them.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep paragraph boundaries as distinct logical payloads through newline-mode automatic reply chunking, let `streaming.block.coalesce` be the sole policy for joining them, and retain the channel text limit only as a hard splitting cap.
