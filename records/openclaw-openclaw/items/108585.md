## Summary

Keep open: the reported chain matches the current empty-response retry design, while existing coverage exercises group and direct silent replies but does not establish the internal subagent-wake invariant. The possible disclosure of injected directives warrants security-aware review rather than routine cleanup.

## What This Changes

Accept intentionally empty internal subagent-completion wake replies when the session’s effective silent-reply policy allows silence, preventing a forced retry from reaching the user channel.

## Best Possible Solution

Derive internal-wake silence from the effective silent-reply policy at the reply-runner boundary, add an end-to-end subagent completion regression proving no retry instruction or outbound delivery under policy="allow", retain retry behavior under policy="disallow", and verify ordinary direct/group turns remain unchanged.
