## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still leave the normal in-process subagent `agent` dispatch on the inherited caller client, and https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99199 is the direct open fix candidate rather than a merged implementation.

## What This Changes

The issue reports a regression where token-auth, channel-triggered `sessions_spawn` fails with `missing scope: operator.write` after upgrading to v2026.6.11.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this issue open until https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99199 or an equivalent narrow `callSubagentGateway` fix lands with proof that token-auth channel `sessions_spawn` succeeds and admin-only cleanup stays admin-scoped.
