## What This Changes

The issue reports a regression where Google Chat-triggered `sessions_spawn` fails with `missing scope: operator.write` on loopback token-auth deployments after upgrading from v2026.6.1 to v2026.6.11.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this issue open until a token-auth `sessions_spawn` fix lands with server/integration or redacted live proof, or until maintainers prove the current release/main no longer reproduces and remove any misleading closing relationship from auth-none-only work.
