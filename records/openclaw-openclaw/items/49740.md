The issue requests configurable per-cron retry controls so failed scheduled jobs can retry after a delay before waiting for their next natural schedule.

Keep this as the canonical per-job cron retry request until maintainers either approve an additive job-level retry policy with explicit global fallback and clear/disable semantics, or decide the shipped global retry policy is the intended product boundary.
