## Summary

Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 have global transient retry/backoff for recurring cron failures, but the requested per-job `openclaw cron add` / `openclaw cron edit` retry-count, retry-delay, and retry-backoff contract is still absent and needs maintainer product/API review.

## What This Changes

The issue requests configurable per-cron retry controls so failed scheduled jobs can retry after transient provider outages before their next natural run.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical per-job cron retry request, then either land a reviewed additive design for job-level retry policy or explicitly decide that the shipped global transient retry behavior is the intended product boundary.
