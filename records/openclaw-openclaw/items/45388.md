## What This Changes

Review and land https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/96252 or an equivalent TUI-side `session.message` consumer with live Gateway/TUI proof; keep the broader bidirectional mirroring request in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/40678.

## Solution Assessment
