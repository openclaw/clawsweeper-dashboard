## Summary

This remains a credible claude-cli lifecycle bug: a successful result can finalize the OpenClaw turn while native background work is still active, making later output and delivery unavailable. The protected maintainer label and directly linked open fix PR require keeping the issue open until that PR is reviewed and merged or replaced.

## What This Changes

Keep claude-cli turns and their delivery capability alive until native background subagents or workflows complete, fail, or reach the existing bounded timeout.

## Best Possible Solution

Land a bounded claude-cli lifecycle fix that consumes the authoritative background-task list, defers only successful interim results for result-bearing task types, preserves post-result output and delivery until drain, and retains immediate errors plus the existing hard turn timeout.
