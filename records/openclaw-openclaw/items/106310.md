## Best Possible Solution

Review and land a bounded dependency-contract-based fix that keeps the claude-cli turn and delivery grant alive for outstanding local_agent and local_workflow tasks, merges interim and continuation output, preserves immediate error handling, and leaves background Bash semantics unchanged.
