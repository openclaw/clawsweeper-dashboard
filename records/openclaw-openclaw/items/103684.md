## Summary

Current main still awaits the serialized reply chain during dispatcher settlement, and that chain directly awaits `beforeDeliver` without a deadline. The transcript-mirroring refactor removes one redundant idle wait but does not resolve the reported non-settling callback, undelivered final reply, or blocked lane.

## What This Changes

Fix an intermittent final-reply delivery stall where a non-settling pre-delivery callback leaves the response undelivered and the channel session blocked.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a bounded, observable pre-delivery contract that records a failed or retryable final outcome, releases active lane ownership, and replays or surfaces the persisted final through its original delivery context without manual state-file editing.
