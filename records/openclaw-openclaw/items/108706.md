## Review Findings

Overall correctness: patch is incorrect

Overall confidence: 0.95

Full review comments:

- **[P1] Use a timeout boundary that does not wait for child exit:** `src/cli/ports.ts:108`
  - body: `execFileSync` does not provide the recovery guarantee claimed here: after its timeout sends the default `SIGTERM`, Node still waits until the child fully exits. A wedged or SIGTERM-handling `lsof`, `fuser`, or `netstat` can therefore keep the CLI blocked indefinitely, including the stuck-I/O scenario this PR intends to fix. Run these inspections through an asynchronous subprocess boundary that rejects after the deadline without waiting for child exit, and cover that behavior with a deliberately non-exiting child.
  - confidence: 0.96
