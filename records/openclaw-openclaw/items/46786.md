Yes. Source inspection gives a high-confidence reproduction path: allowed elevated sessions fall back to on, omitted exec.elevated inherits active elevation, and active elevation resolves sandboxed auto exec to gateway.

## Solution Assessment

Unclear pending maintainer decision. Defaulting the implicit elevated level to off is the narrowest fix for the silent sandbox bypass, but changing /elevated ask semantics is a broader security/product contract choice.
