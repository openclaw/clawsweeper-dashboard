## Summary

This PR targets a real restart-budget defect, but the unconditional cap check conflicts with the existing two-pass pending-recovery contract and can prevent a channel from completing recovery when the hourly limit is one. Likely related people: @vincentkoc (high confidence, introduced the pending-recovery path), @rstar327 (high confidence, introduced restart-budget configuration), and @Takhoffman (medium confidence, adjacent co-author).

## What This Changes

The PR makes pending channel restart continuations independently consume and be checked against the hourly restart budget.

## Best Possible Solution

Treat the pending continuation as completion of an already-budgeted logical recovery, while separately bounding genuinely repeated unresolved recovery cycles.
