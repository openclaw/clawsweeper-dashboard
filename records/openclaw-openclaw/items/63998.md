Rationale: The remaining fix may preserve, truncate, quarantine, skip, reset, or migrate user transcripts, and that choice affects recovery behavior, user data preservation, and unattended service availability.

Options:

- **Quarantine and degrade by default (recommended):** Skip the oversized active transcript on startup, preserve it for doctor/manual recovery, and let the gateway continue without re-entering the crash loop.
