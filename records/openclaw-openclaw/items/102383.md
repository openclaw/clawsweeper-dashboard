Use one durable Gateway-owned agent-deletion state machine that records intent, disables all agent-owned jobs exactly once, resumes safely after partial failure, and prevents local deletion from bypassing scheduler cleanup; then prove fresh install, failure, retry, and successful removal in a live setup.
