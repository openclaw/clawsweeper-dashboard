## Best Possible Solution

Land the bounded-read hardening with a loop that reads until EOF or the cap, after maintainers accept, tune, or document the chosen batch-file limit.

## Solution Assessment

No, not as submitted. The parser boundary is the right place to cap memory, but the implementation should handle short reads and the new hard public cap needs maintainer acceptance or adjustment.
