## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Land the parser-level bounded read only after maintainers accept or tune the public file-size limit and align docs/help with the chosen cap.

## Reproduction Assessment

## Solution Assessment

No, not fully. The parser-level bounded read is the right layer, but the hard public 1 MiB cap needs maintainer acceptance, tuning, or documentation before merge.
