## Summary

Keep this issue open: OpenClaw already intends successful message-tool sends to be mirrored into the routed session transcript, so a current Discord failure would be a real regression, but the report does not identify the exact version, target form, DM-scope configuration, destination session, or transcript entry inspected and also combines OpenClaw-owned sends with external bot/webhook posts that have different trust and ownership semantics. ([github.com](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/50092))

## What This Changes

Ensure Discord DM posts produced by OpenClaw tools or isolated scheduled runs are represented consistently in the appropriate session history, while clearly defining the treatment of external bot and webhook posts.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep isolated-run transcripts as their own lineage, mirror each successful OpenClaw-owned proactive send exactly once into the resolved destination conversation with explicit provenance, and leave external bot/webhook posts outside agent history unless they pass the normal authorized inbound pipeline; expose enough route metadata to explain the distinction in history and diagnostics.
