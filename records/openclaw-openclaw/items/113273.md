## What This Changes

Adds visible WhatsApp and Signal messages reporting the canonical approval outcome when an operator’s reaction loses an approval-resolution race.

## System Context

WhatsApp and Signal inbound monitors receive approval reactions and ask their channel-specific resolver to apply the decision through the gateway. When another operator already resolved the approval, this change sends a best-effort notice back to the affected conversation instead of silently consuming the reaction.
