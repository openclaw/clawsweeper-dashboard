## Best Possible Solution

## Solution Assessment

Yes, pending maintainer policy acceptance. The shared reset archive helper is the narrowest maintainable owner because both reset paths route through it; a narrower outbound-only or shorter-retention policy is the safer alternative only if maintainers reject retaining full trajectory archives.
