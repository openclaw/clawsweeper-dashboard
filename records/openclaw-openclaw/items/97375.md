## Summary

Keep the doctor-owned migration, but only classify a ref as orphaned when its provider is definitively unavailable or a provider-owned validator rejects it; preserve explicit and dynamic refs absent from catalog snapshots, and remove optional stale refs rather than silently routing them to an arbitrary provider.
