## Summary

Keep open as the canonical Telegram Business support tracker. Current main and the latest release still allow delivery-level Business update names but do not register Business message/connection handlers, do not key Business updates for dedupe/sequencing, and do not define the outbound authority model needed for safe replies.

## What This Changes

Support Telegram Business-connected personal chats in the bundled Telegram plugin by processing `business_connection` and `business_message` updates and replying with the correct `business_connection_id`.

## Best Possible Solution

Ship a maintainer-approved Telegram plugin implementation with per-account durable Business connection state, disconnect cleanup, Business-aware dedupe and sequencing, safe outbound `business_connection_id` handling, docs, focused tests, and live Telegram proof.
