## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still allow Telegram Business update names through grammY defaults, but they do not register `business_message` or `business_connection` handlers or persist/apply Business connection state for outbound replies.

## What This Changes

Request support for Telegram Business bot updates so OpenClaw can receive and reply to personal-chat `business_message` events with the required Business connection context.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical tracker until a refreshed Telegram plugin implementation handles Business connect/disconnect state per account, routes `business_message` through the inbound pipeline, updates dedupe/sequencing, applies outbound `business_connection_id` safely, and adds tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.

Yes, at source level: current main allows Telegram Business update names through grammY defaults but has no Business message or connection handlers and no Business payload keying path. I did not run a live Telegram Business bot in this read-only review.

## Solution Assessment
