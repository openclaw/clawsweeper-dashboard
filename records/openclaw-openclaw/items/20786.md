## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still only expose Telegram Business update names through grammY defaults and adjacent outbound callback metadata; they do not route `business_message` or `business_connection` updates through the Telegram runtime, keying, docs, or tests.

## What This Changes

Request to support Telegram Business `business_message` and `business_connection` updates so Business-connected personal chats can be received and answered through the Telegram plugin.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical tracker until a refreshed Telegram plugin implementation handles Business connection state per account, routes `business_message` through the inbound pipeline, updates dedupe/sequencing, applies outbound `business_connection_id` safely, and adds docs, tests, and live Telegram proof.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes, at source level: current main allows the Business update names through grammY defaults, but has no Business message or connection handlers and no Business payload keying path, so delivered Business updates are not routed into OpenClaw.

## Solution Assessment

No, the original allowed-updates-only proposal is too narrow. The maintainable solution needs runtime handlers, account-scoped Business connection state, outbound `business_connection_id` handling, dedupe/sequencing updates, docs, tests, and live Telegram proof.
