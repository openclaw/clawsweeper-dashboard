## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still allow Telegram Business update names through grammY defaults but do not process `business_message` or `business_connection`, and the remaining implementation needs Telegram-owner review because it affects personal-DM access, outbound authority, account scoping, and live Telegram proof.

## What This Changes

Add Telegram Business Bot support so Business-connected personal-chat updates are routed through OpenClaw and replies can use the required Business connection context.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical tracker until a refreshed Telegram plugin implementation handles Business connect/disconnect state per account, routes business_message through the inbound pipeline, updates dedupe/sequencing, applies outbound business_connection_id safely, and adds focused tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.

Yes, at source level: current main permits the Business update names through grammY defaults but has no Business message or connection handlers and no Business payload keying path, so delivered Business updates are not routed into OpenClaw.

## Solution Assessment

No, the allowed-updates-only shape is too narrow. The maintainable fix needs runtime handlers, account-scoped Business connection state, safe outbound business_connection_id handling, dedupe/sequencing updates, tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.
