## Summary

Keep this open as the canonical Telegram Business support tracker. Current main and v2026.6.11 still allow Telegram Business update delivery through grammY defaults, but they do not register Business message/connection handlers, do not key Business payloads for dedupe/sequencing, and have not resolved the outbound `business_connection_id` security boundary.

## What This Changes

The issue requests Telegram Business Bot support by accepting and handling Telegram `business_message` and `business_connection` updates, including outbound reply support through `business_connection_id`.

## Best Possible Solution

Ship a maintainer-approved Telegram plugin implementation that handles Business connect/disconnect state per account, routes `business_message` through existing DM authorization and inbound processing, updates dedupe/sequential keys, applies `business_connection_id` only with correct authority, and documents setup with live Telegram proof.
