## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still allow Telegram Business update names through grammY defaults, but the Telegram plugin does not handle business_message or business_connection updates, key Business payloads, or document the Business flow. ## What This Changes

Request support for Telegram Business bot updates by receiving business_connection/business_message updates and routing Business messages through Telegram channel replies with business_connection_id handling.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical tracker until a refreshed Telegram plugin implementation handles Business connect/disconnect state per account, routes business_message through the existing inbound pipeline, updates dedupe and sequencing, applies outbound business_connection_id safely, and adds focused tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.

Yes, at source level: current main includes Business update names through grammY defaults, but has no business_message or business_connection handlers and no Business payload keying path, so delivered Business updates are not routed into OpenClaw.

## Solution Assessment

No, an allowed-updates-only fix is too narrow. The maintainable path needs runtime handlers, account-scoped connection state, safe outbound business_connection_id handling, dedupe/sequencing updates, tests, docs, and live Telegram proof.
