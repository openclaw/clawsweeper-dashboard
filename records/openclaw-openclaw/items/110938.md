- `merge-risk: 🚨 message-delivery`: The new late-success notice can be delivered to the wrong conversation, and failed sends can receive a contradictory delivery-delay notice.

## Summary

Keep this protected-label Reef feature open for maintainer direction and repair: the proposed overdue notice closes a real silent-delivery gap, but its late-success follow-up can route to the wrong session and its timer starts before relay acceptance is known.

## What This Changes

The branch adds persisted timestamps and a periodic Reef sweep that notifies an owner about unconfirmed outbound deliveries, then sends a late-delivery follow-up when a receipt eventually arrives.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep overdue tracking in the Reef owner plugin, establish notification eligibility only after relay acceptance is known, and send both delay and completion notices through the same peer-scoped wake-up route with real after-fix delivery proof.
