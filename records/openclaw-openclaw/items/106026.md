Full review comments:

- **[P2] Preserve preamble identity so retractions clear headlines:** `extensions/discord/src/monitor/message-handler.draft-preview.ts:207`
  - body: `pushPreambleItemEvent` drops `itemId` when forwarding a headline. If the same preamble item is later retracted with empty text, the compositor cannot distinguish that retraction and can retain a headline the model withdrew, especially while the five-second gate is still hidden. Pass and retain the item identity, then clear only the matching headline on an empty update.
  - confidence: 0.96
- **[P2] Dispose narrator retries when the turn finalizes:** `src/auto-reply/reply/progress-narrator.ts:29`
  - body: The visibility and preamble-freshness retries can remain armed after the final answer because narrator lifetime is not tied to finalization. The timer can then invoke the utility model for text that the finalized compositor rejects, preserving the paid completion this PR intends to eliminate. Add a disposal/finalization signal or require a live draft immediately before generation.
  - confidence: 0.94
- **[P2] Cancel the Teams progress gate during finalization:** `extensions/msteams/src/reply-stream-controller.ts:146`
  - body: After making the shared gate purely delayed, a fast Teams turn can finalize while its five-second timer remains pending. The callback can subsequently call `stream.update()` on a completed or closed stream, yielding a stale Working update or a close-time error. Cancel or permanently guard the gate from the controller's finalization path.
  - confidence: 0.95
- **[P2] Count Discord commentary only after sanitization succeeds:** `extensions/discord/src/monitor/message-handler.draft-preview.ts:212`
  - body: This increments the receipt for any non-empty raw preamble before `pushCommentaryProgress` applies the delivery sanitizer. A control-only or silent preamble is discarded from the draft but still produces a final 💬 count. Share the sanitizer or have the compositor report acceptance before calling `noteCommentary`.
  - confidence: 0.97
