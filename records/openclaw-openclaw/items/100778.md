The issue reports that failed preflight compaction provider calls can terminate a pending reply and leave the Composer disabled until refresh.

## Best Possible Solution

Land one focused runtime fix that skips only approved retryable preflight failures, such as timeout, 429, and 5xx, with 400/401/403 negative tests and real web-chat or terminal proof.
