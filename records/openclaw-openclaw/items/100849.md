Full review comments:

- **[P2] Do not finish system.run before the child exits:** `src/node-host/invoke.ts:354-356`
  - body: These handlers call `finalize` as soon as stdout or stderr emits `error`. `executeSystemRunPhase` treats `runCommand` resolving as command completion, so a stream error from a still-running child reports a failed result, clears the timer, and leaves the process running after OpenClaw says it finished. Please record the output-stream error, terminate or wait for the child, and resolve through the process exit/close path.
