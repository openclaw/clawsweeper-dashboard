## Best Possible Solution

Review and land https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/99747, or a replacement that puts post-turn-accept resource acquisition under one acquisition-aware cleanup/disposer boundary with focused setup-throw regression coverage.

## Reproduction Assessment
