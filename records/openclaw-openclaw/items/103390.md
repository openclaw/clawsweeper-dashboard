## Summary

The latest head resolves the two prior ClawSweeper blockers by removing GC adoption and routing normal registration through the atomic claim, but the create-side orphan signature still cannot distinguish a crashed creator from a creator actively running setup. That leaves a merge-blocking race where a same-name retry can provision concurrently and remove the first caller’s worktree on failure.

**Likely related people**
- `steipete` — high confidence; introduced the managed-worktree lifecycle.
- `vincentkoc` — medium confidence; most recent current-main contributor to the service path.

## What This Changes

The PR makes same-name worktree creation adopt exact crash-orphan paths, atomically claims live registry paths, and adds real-git/SQLite recovery and concurrency tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Establish a lifecycle-owned creating marker or same-name lock before the worktree becomes adoptable, and permit recovery only after that creator is proven abandoned so provisioning and destructive cleanup remain single-owner.
