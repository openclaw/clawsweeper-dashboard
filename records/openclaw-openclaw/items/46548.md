## Label Justifications

- `P2`: The issue affects confusing and potentially unsafe tool-failure reporting in agent/channel workflows, but it needs product policy and has bounded blast radius rather than an emergency outage.
- `impact:security`: The requested policy must prevent raw failure reasons from leaking paths, commands, provider output, or other sensitive operational details into chat.
- `impact:message-loss`: Incorrect standalone failure warnings and missing recovered confirmations can cause users to receive misleading channel messages about agent work state.

## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still omit ordinary compact failure reasons and clear retry state internally without a visible recovered or retry-success confirmation. Adjacent fixes improved heartbeat exec details, Discord stale warnings, mutating false-positive suppression, and exec labels, but they do not resolve the operator-safety request.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to include failure reasons in chat-visible tool error warnings and confirm when a later retry or recovery succeeds.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a channel-safe tool-failure notification policy that shows redacted actionable reasons when appropriate and emits or suppresses recovered-state notices based on matching tool/failure fingerprints and side-effect risk.
