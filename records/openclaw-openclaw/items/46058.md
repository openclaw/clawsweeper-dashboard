## Summary

## What This Changes

The issue asks whether OpenClaw should discuss a chat-first official Android surface or selectively upstream validated Android UX, media, onboarding, and connectivity improvements from an external fork.

## Best Possible Solution

Android owners should choose the official launch/navigation/media scope first, then invite one or two focused real-device-proof Android PR slices while keeping broad mobile design in the RFC venue and Android Gateway hosting under its own issue.
