Keep open. Current main and v2026.6.11 still lack the requested trusted-owner Telegram group approval bypass, and deciding whether to add it changes an approval/security boundary plus config surface.

Design a disabled-by-default, maintainer-approved trusted-owner policy that composes with Telegram group authorization, shared exec/elevated approval policy, tool-class gates, destructive-operation handling, audit records, docs, and Telegram proof.

Options:

- **Add Telegram-only shortcut:** Implement a narrow Telegram config, accepting faster ergonomics but higher risk of policy drift from shared approvals and elevated execution.
