## Best Possible Solution

Add a bounded explicit `openclaw skills check` or Doctor orphan diagnostic that excludes all configured/trusted roots and gives move, install, or configure remediation; avoid startup or home-wide scans unless maintainers intentionally accept that tradeoff.
