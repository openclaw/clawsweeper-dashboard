## What This Changes

The branch puts Gmail’s `gog` watcher in its own Unix process group and routes shutdown through the shared process-tree terminator so credential-helper descendants are stopped too.

## System Context

Gmail hooks launch `gog gmail watch serve` as a long-running subprocess after registering a watch. On shutdown, the hook must stop that watcher and any helper processes without signaling the OpenClaw host process.
