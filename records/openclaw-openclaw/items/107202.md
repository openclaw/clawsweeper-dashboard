## What This Changes

Treat declined or pre-execution exec results as blocked rather than failed, while omitting nonexistent timing values from command-output telemetry.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve `declined` as a structured blocked outcome from ingestion through telemetry and chat rendering, omit execution-only fields when no process started, and land focused regression coverage across classifier, summary, payload, and activity-event surfaces.
