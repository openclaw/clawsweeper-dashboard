## What This Changes

Adds the oldest active run’s start time to channel snapshots and uses that fixed age to bound the disconnected-channel busy-health override.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the internal oldest-run timestamp design, then land it after redacted live or Crabbox evidence shows a blocked disconnected channel becomes `stuck`, reaches the monitor recovery path, and normal overlapping work remains healthy.
