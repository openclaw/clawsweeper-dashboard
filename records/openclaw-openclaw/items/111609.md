## Summary

Keep this PR open: the chunked reader closes the TOCTOU allocation gap, but the new unconditional 32 MiB cap still makes already-existing oversized Reef audit or replay stores unreadable without an approved recovery path.

## What This Changes

The PR replaces Reef JSONL `readFile` calls with a 32 MiB chunked reader and adds tests that oversized audit and replay stores reject with `RangeError`.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the bounded streaming reader, but pair it with a Reef-owned, explicitly approved retention or recovery design that preserves the relevant audit and replay invariants and proves both fresh-store and upgrade-store behavior.
