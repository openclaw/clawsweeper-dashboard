## What This Changes

Maintainers should choose one explicit contract: either approve a guarded Discord-only same-name fallback after explicit bindings miss, with diagnostics, docs, regression tests, and live Discord proof, or keep explicit bindings as the contract and add doctor/status guidance for risky missing bindings.
