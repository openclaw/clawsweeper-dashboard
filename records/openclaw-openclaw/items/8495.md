## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still block or drop unauthorized WhatsApp group traffic, but they do not auto-leave unapproved groups, emit a WhatsApp group-join event, or expose a WhatsApp leave-group action. The related group-pairing, Telegram, and per-group WhatsApp allowlist items overlap but do not supersede this WhatsApp-specific security feature.

## What This Changes

Request opt-in WhatsApp behavior to leave or notify on unauthorized group additions and expose a manual WhatsApp group leave action.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a product/security-reviewed WhatsApp plugin contract that separates group admission from sender authorization, adds opt-in schema/docs/tests, authorizes any destructive leave action per account and target, and coordinates owner notification with the broader group-pairing design.
