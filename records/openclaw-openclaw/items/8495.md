## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still deny unauthorized WhatsApp group traffic without removing the account from the group, and no related item owns this WhatsApp-specific membership-control work. The feature is feasible with the pinned dependency, but its destructive authorization boundary and public event/tool scope require maintainer direction.

## What This Changes

Add opt-in WhatsApp handling to leave unauthorized groups, emit a group-join notification or event, and optionally expose a manual leave action.

## Best Possible Solution

Extend the WhatsApp listener with exact self-add classification, compare the group JID against the effective configured groups map, and perform an opt-in deterministic leave plus owner notification without routing untrusted group content to the agent.

- **Broader hook and tool API:** Define a public group-join event and separately authorized leave action in coordination with cross-channel group pairing.
- **Retain deny-only behavior:** Continue dropping unauthorized traffic and document manual membership cleanup through WhatsApp.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes at source level: current main and v2026.6.11 drop unregistered group traffic and receive detailed participant events, but do not classify account self-adds, notify owners, or invoke the available leave operation. No live paired WhatsApp group-add scenario was run during this read-only review.

## Solution Assessment

No, not as one combined change. The narrowest maintainable first step is a WhatsApp-owned, JID-based unknown-group policy; a general hook and manual destructive action should require separate approval and authorization design.
