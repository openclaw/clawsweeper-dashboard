## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still block or drop unauthorized WhatsApp group traffic, but they do not leave unapproved groups, emit a WhatsApp group-join event, or expose a WhatsApp leave action. The remaining work is security-sensitive product design, not conservative cleanup.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a product/security-reviewed WhatsApp plugin contract that separates group admission from sender authorization, adds opt-in schema/docs/tests, authorizes destructive leave behavior per account and target, and coordinates owner notification with the broader group-pairing design.
