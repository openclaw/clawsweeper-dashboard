## What This Changes

Adds an opt-in `maxToolCallingRounds` limit at agent-default, subagent-default, and per-agent scope, enforced by agent-core and the embedded runner with a tool-free exhaustion closeout, structured metadata, docs, and regression coverage.

Keep one opt-in `maxToolCallingRounds` setting, but land it only after maintainers define the permanent harness contract—prefer capability-aware enforcement or safe implicit fallback to built-in OpenClaw while preserving explicit-harness intent—and after exact-head live proof and focused regression files demonstrate the full tool-free closeout path.
