## What This Changes

Removes the QQBot token-refresh sleep abort listener after normal timer completion and adds regression coverage for repeated refresh cycles.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep abort-listener ownership inside the QQBot sleep helper so every normal completion detaches its listener while abort cancellation remains once-only and regression-tested.
