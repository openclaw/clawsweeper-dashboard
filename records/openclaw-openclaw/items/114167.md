## What This Changes

Adds an opt-in `status_changed` Workboard notification that records card lifecycle transitions with transition metadata for notification subscribers.

## System Context

The Workboard plugin updates cards through its store and persists their state in a keyed store or SQLite. Notification subscribers later read the stored cards, filter events by subscription scope and requested kinds, and deliver the resulting stream to downstream consumers.
