## What This Changes

Adds an opt-in `status_changed` notification kind to the Workboard plugin, persistently recording card status transitions and exposing them to filtered subscribers, tools, and documentation.

## System Context

Workboard stores cards and their lifecycle transitions, then reads those records to deliver filtered notifications to subscribers. This change adds a replayable transition event for downstream consumers while retaining the existing terminal-event stream for subscriptions that do not opt in.
