## Summary

The patch appears technically sound and atomically fixes the linked cross-plugin mutation bug, but merge should wait for maintainer approval of the fail-closed policy for shipped ownerless rows and after-fix proof from a real Gateway plugin-runtime setup.

## What This Changes

The PR enforces plugin ownership in `sessions.patch` under the session-store writer lock, stamps ownership on patch-created rows, and adds regression coverage for owned, foreign, ownerless, and racing session states.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the atomic transaction-selected ownership check and creation-time owner stamping, explicitly approve or migrate the legacy ownerless-row boundary, then require a rebased live Gateway proof covering foreign rejection, owner success, new-row ownership, and ownerless rejection.
