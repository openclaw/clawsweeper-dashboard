## Summary

Keep open: the reported cross-plugin authorization gap is real and the deployed proof is strong, but the fail-closed legacy-row policy needs explicit security-owner approval and the patch makes plugin-owned sessions unusable after `sessions.reset` strips their ownership.

## What This Changes

Adds plugin-runtime ownership checks to `sessions.patch`, stamps `pluginOwnerId` on newly created rows, and adds authorization-race and persistence coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Use one canonical exact-owner authorization utility across create, patch, delete, and reset; perform checks against the authoritative locked row, preserve `pluginOwnerId` when reset rebuilds a session, test fresh and upgraded rows, and document the explicitly approved policy for legacy ownerless state.
