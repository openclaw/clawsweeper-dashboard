Fix both manual-scroll-away Jump-state bugs, keep the change local to native UI/controller state with focused tests, then land only after maintainer acceptance of the reader-managed scroll UX.

The best repair is to derive newer-content state from the viewport on both native surfaces and cover that case in tests.
