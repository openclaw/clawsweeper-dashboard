Keep open. Current main now covers much of the organization layer with custom collapsible session groups and whole-session forks, but it still lacks structural selected-message split/move, seed/copy from chosen context, and multi-target composer routing. Those remaining behaviors require explicit product and protocol decisions rather than conservative cleanup.

## What This Changes
