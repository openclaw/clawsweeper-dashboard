## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still only mitigate this Feishu DM session-continuity bug. Account-scoped DM keys are available and documented, but direct DM rows remain normal maintenance candidates and Feishu inbound direct peer IDs are still accepted without Feishu-specific malformed open_id validation or repair.

## What This Changes

The issue reports Feishu direct-message sessions being rebuilt or forked after gateway restarts because direct-session rows can be pruned and duplicate or malformed open_id-based keys can be adopted in multi-account setups.

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt a maintainer-approved Feishu DM continuity plan: keep per-account-channel-peer as the documented multi-account workaround, add bounded protection for recent human direct-message sessions, and repair or reject malformed Feishu direct IDs through Feishu-owned validation and doctor/migration handling.
