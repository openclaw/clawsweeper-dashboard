## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still have a Google Chat path where a threaded typing placeholder can become the final reply even after `replyToMode: "off"` removes the final payload reply target, and https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/89790 is the open closing fix candidate.

## What This Changes

Google Chat should honor `channels.googlechat.replyToMode: "off"` by preventing replies and typing-placeholder updates from attaching to inbound threads.

## Best Possible Solution

Review and land the narrow Google Chat delivery fix in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/89790, then close this issue from the merged commit.
