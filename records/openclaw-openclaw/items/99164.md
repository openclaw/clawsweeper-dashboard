- The result-payload classifier can still classify `refusal` without access to `refusalFallback`, which can route provider-blocked prompts to another model even when the default-off policy says they should be terminal.
- Routing provider safety/content-filter refusals across models or providers remains a maintainer product/security decision.
- The proof is synthetic classifier/test output rather than a redacted real OpenClaw/provider run showing a refusal moving through a configured fallback chain.
