## Summary

Keep open for maintainer/security review. Current main and v2026.6.11 already reject the simple mismatched attacker-origin POST described and audit exposed Host-header fallback as critical, but the remaining dangerous fallback is a real security-boundary/product decision rather than a safe cleanup close.

## What This Changes

The issue asks OpenClaw to remove or harden `gateway.controlUi.dangerouslyAllowHostHeaderOriginFallback` because Host-header origin fallback may weaken CSRF protection on network-exposed gateways.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this issue open until gateway/security owners explicitly choose whether to keep the dangerous fallback as an audited operator policy, harden it with defenses such as `Sec-Fetch-Site` or tokens, or remove it with a compatibility-aware migration path.
