Keep this as the canonical updater workflow request and design a maintainer-approved, opt-in core workflow on the safe update.run or external-updater path with schedule/timezone, confirm or notify-only modes, explicit silent mode, durable restart state, channel targeting, and old/new version plus changelog and health reporting.
