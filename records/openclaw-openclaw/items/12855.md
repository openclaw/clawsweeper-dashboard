## What This Changes

Request a built-in `update.auto` workflow with configurable schedule/timezone, notify-only/confirm/silent modes, channel-targeted post-update notifications, and old/new version, changelog, and health reporting.

Keep this issue open as the canonical updater engine/workflow tracker; design a maintainer-approved opt-in workflow on top of existing `update.auto` and supervised `update.run`, with schedule/timezone policy, notify-only/confirm/silent modes, safe channel targeting, durable restart state, old/new version reporting, changelog or release-link context, health summary, docs, and focused tests.
