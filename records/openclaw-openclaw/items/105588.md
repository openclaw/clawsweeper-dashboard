## Risks / Open Questions

- Present-state inventory reconstruction and historical exact-turn evidence have different lifetimes; combining them into one unlabeled availability result could overstate what a tool could actually do in the selected turn.
- Trajectory context may include prompts, messages, tool descriptions, parameter schemas, workspace paths, and other sensitive fields, so a report needs stricter authorization and field allowlisting than the general trajectory bundle.
- A cross-runtime report could become a competing capability engine if it rediscovers plugins, policies, or tools instead of consuming prepared facts from their current owners.
- The proposed Linux publication, version-pointer, rollback, retention, and live-report approval model is not yet an established OpenClaw product contract.
