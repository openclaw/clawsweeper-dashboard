Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still lack a WhatsApp or shared channel/account hard no-outbound mode, and the related open/closed items are partial or unmerged rather than a safe canonical implementation.

## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the canonical no-outbound channel/account design issue until maintainers choose the contract, then land focused enforcement with docs, config/schema coverage, regression tests, and live transport proof while tracking no-agent hooks-only ingestion separately.
