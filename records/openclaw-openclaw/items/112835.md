## What This Changes

Investigate assistant voice replies that are truncated when played through CarPlay while the same generated audio completes on the iPhone.

## System Context

OpenClaw’s iOS Talk experience turns assistant responses into playback audio and sends it through the iPhone audio session. CarPlay changes the output route, so route changes, interruptions, or player lifecycle handling can affect what reaches the vehicle speakers.
