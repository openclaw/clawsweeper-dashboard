## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still lack a structural per-channel or per-spawn policy for suppressing sub-agent completion announcements, and the related open items overlap without superseding this channel-specific product/API decision.

## What This Changes

Request a config or policy surface to suppress or reroute sub-agent completion announcements per channel so raw completion output does not clutter Telegram or other chat sessions.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one backward-compatible completion-delivery policy that defaults to current behavior and can suppress or internalize selected channel or spawn completions before active wake, requester handoff, and direct fallback paths.
