## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still lack a structural per-channel or per-spawn suppression policy for sub-agent completion announcements, and the related reports overlap without superseding this channel-specific product/API decision.

## What This Changes

Requests a public per-channel or per-spawn policy to suppress or reroute sub-agent completion announcements so user-facing chats do not receive automatic raw sub-agent output.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one backward-compatible completion-delivery policy that defaults to current behavior and can mark selected channel or spawn completions as suppressed or internal-only before active wake, requester handoff, and direct text/media fallback paths.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes for the missing capability. Source inspection shows current main and v2026.6.11 expose no per-channel or per-spawn sub-agent announce suppression policy while delivery still resolves external targets and fallback sends.

## Solution Assessment

Unclear as proposed. A per-channel key is plausible, but maintainers should choose a unified completion-delivery policy that composes with global suppression, parent routing, hooks, and fallback reliability.
