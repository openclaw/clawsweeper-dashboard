## Summary

Keep open: current main and the latest checked release still lack a structural per-channel or per-spawn policy for suppressing sub-agent completion announcements, and the related open items overlap without replacing this channel-specific config/API decision.

## What This Changes

Request a per-channel or more general config/tool policy to suppress or reroute sub-agent completion announcements so channel chats do not receive raw worker output.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one backward-compatible completion-delivery policy that defaults to current behavior and can mark selected channel or spawn completions as suppressed or internal-only before active wake, requester-agent handoff, and direct fallback paths.
