## Summary

Keep open: current main still lacks a structural per-channel or per-spawn policy for suppressing or rerouting sub-agent completion announcements, and related open items overlap without superseding this channel-specific product/API decision.

## What This Changes

The issue requests a per-channel config or broader completion-delivery policy that suppresses or reroutes sub-agent completion announcements away from selected chat channels.

## Best Possible Solution

Define one backward-compatible completion-delivery policy that defaults to current behavior and can suppress or internalize selected channel or spawn completions before active wake, requester handoff, and direct fallback paths.
