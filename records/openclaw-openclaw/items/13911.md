## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still lack structural control over sub-agent completion announcements, and reports confirm the unwanted external delivery across Telegram and iMessage. The older suppression issue overlaps substantially, but it does not resolve whether selected-channel matching belongs in the canonical policy; prior maintainer-facing review also explicitly retained this issue for that product/API decision.

## What This Changes

Add a structural policy that can suppress or reroute sub-agent completion announcements for selected channels while preserving ordinary interactive replies.

## Best Possible Solution

Add one backward-compatible, agent-owned completion-audience policy that defaults to current channel delivery, supports explicit internal-only or suppressed behavior with deliberately chosen global, per-spawn, or channel matching, and gates every primary and fallback outbound path.

Question: What public completion-delivery contract should own suppression: a generic agent-level audience policy with optional per-spawn and channel matching, or separate channel-specific settings?

Rationale: The missing behavior is clear, but configuration ownership and granularity affect compatibility, channel boundaries, fallback semantics, and overlap with the open parent-routing and extension-hook requests.

Likely owner: steipete

Owner confidence: high

Options:

- **Unified audience policy (recommended):** Define one generic sub-agent completion policy under the agent/runtime boundary, default it to current external delivery, and allow narrowly scoped per-spawn or channel matching.
- **Global and per-spawn only:** Keep channel adapters free of completion policy and support only a global default plus explicit spawn overrides.
- **Channel-specific settings:** Add independent channel configuration keys, accepting duplicated policy and greater configuration-surface growth.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes at source level: current main exposes no structural suppression or audience control, and the discussion provides consistent Telegram and iMessage observations. This read-only review did not perform a live current-main channel run.

## Solution Assessment

No, a standalone Telegram or channel-specific boolean is not the best long-term solution; the narrow maintainable direction is one generic completion-audience contract that covers primary delivery and fallback while preserving current defaults.
