## Summary

Keep open: current main has provider-specific recovery for iMessage, Telegram webhook spooling, and durable receive primitives, but Slack/Discord-style post-restart history catch-up remains missing and needs maintainer product direction.

## What This Changes

Request a post-startup inbound catch-up mechanism that scans channel history after gateway restarts and re-enqueues messages missed while providers were offline.

## Best Possible Solution

Design a core/plugin catch-up contract with provider-owned implementations, durable idempotency, per-provider policy, and an explicit config/default choice; let partial queue-drain and followup-persistence PRs land or close on their own merits.

Options:

- **Define a provider-owned catch-up contract (recommended):** Add a core/plugin seam for restart catch-up and require each provider implementation to prove gating, idempotency, rate limits, and upgrade-safe config behavior.
- **Keep fixes provider-specific:** Continue landing targeted recovery work for iMessage, Telegram, followup queues, and individual Slack/Discord gaps without committing to one cross-channel contract yet.
- **Decline generic catch-up:** Document that only transports with durable replay support recover missed offline messages and leave other channels to user or plugin tooling.
