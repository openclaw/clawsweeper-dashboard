## Summary

Keep open: current main has provider-specific reliability for iMessage and Telegram plus adjacent restart/queue mitigation work, but Slack and Discord still do not perform post-startup history catch-up for messages missed while the gateway or provider was offline.

## What This Changes

Requests a configurable post-startup inbound catch-up mechanism that queries channel history after gateway restart and re-injects missed messages into normal handling.

## Best Possible Solution

Define a provider-owned missed-inbound replay/cursor contract first, then pilot Slack and Discord with durable dedupe, live-channel proof, and policy parity with normal inbound handling.
