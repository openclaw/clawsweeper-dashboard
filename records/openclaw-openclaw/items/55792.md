Keep open: current main has iMessage-specific downtime replay and adjacent queued-message restart work, but it still lacks the requested cross-channel catch-up for inbound messages sent while Slack, Discord, or other providers are offline.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep this as the cross-channel missed-inbound catch-up tracker, choose a provider-owned replay/cursor contract first, then land narrow Slack/Discord implementations with durable dedupe, policy parity, upgrade-safe config, and live channel proof.
