## Summary

## What This Changes

The PR adds an embedded-runner timeout notice for no-payload timed-out turns, a pure decision helper, and regression coverage.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the terminal-boundary timeout notice, but suppress it for delivered `message_tool_only` source replies and cover that case before merge.

## Reproduction Assessment

Yes, at source level. A timed-out tool-execution attempt with no assistant text reproduces the silent path, and the remaining finding is source-reproducible with `didDeliverSourceReplyViaMessageTool: true`, no mirror payloads, and no ordinary messaging-tool evidence.

## Solution Assessment

No, not quite. The embedded-runner terminal boundary is the right layer, but the gate needs to preserve delivered source-reply evidence before this is the best fix.
