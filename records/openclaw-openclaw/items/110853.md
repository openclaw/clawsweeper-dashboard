## Summary

## What This Changes

The branch raises the iMessage send timeout floor from 150 to 180 seconds and prevents `probeTimeoutMs` from shortening an outbound send below that floor.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the dedicated send floor and its single-RPC design, then rebase onto current `main`, reconfirm the imsg timing contract, and merge only after a maintainer explicitly accepts the bounded extra failure latency to avoid ambiguous successful sends and caller retries.
