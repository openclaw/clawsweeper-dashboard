## Review Findings

Overall correctness: patch is incorrect

Overall confidence: 0.88

Full review comments:

- **[P1] Bound the post-cap stream read:** `src/agents/tools/web-shared.ts:279`
  - body: After `bytesRead` reaches `maxBytes`, this waits for another `reader.read()` before returning. That violates the existing bounded-read/endless-stream behavior: a response can deliver exactly the cap and then keep the stream open without another chunk or EOF, leaving `web_fetch` waiting instead of returning a capped result. The fix needs a bounded overread/content-length contract or another non-stalling way to distinguish exact EOF from overflow.
  - confidence: 0.89
- **[P2] Keep skipping empty post-cap chunks:** `src/agents/tools/web-shared.ts:280`
  - body: The normal loop explicitly ignores empty chunks before applying byte-limit logic, but this lookahead treats one empty chunk after the cap as completion and breaks. A stream that emits `maxBytes`, then an empty chunk, then more data would be reported complete even though data was omitted; the overread path should preserve the same empty-chunk invariant as the main loop.
  - confidence: 0.82
