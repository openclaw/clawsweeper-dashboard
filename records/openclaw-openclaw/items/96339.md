The PR makes reply usage-line resolution async and appends active-provider quota summaries to `/usage tokens` and `/usage full` reply footers when provider usage data is available.

Restore the token-use docs, keep provider-specific fetching inside the existing provider-usage/plugin seams, and land only after maintainers accept the bounded per-reply lookup or ask for cached/preloaded quota state.
