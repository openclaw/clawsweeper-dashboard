## What This Changes

The PR keeps LINE’s 15-second 202/readiness deadline and adds a separate 120-second wall-clock deadline that aborts a slow-dripping successful media response body.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the separate readiness and body budgets, but land the 120-second body cap only after the LINE-path owner confirms that bounded failure is the intended delivery policy for valid but very slow inbound media.
