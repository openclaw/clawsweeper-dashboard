## Summary

Keep open: current main and v2026.6.11 still allow paths-only browser upload arming and the agent client still posts `/hooks/file-chooser` with a fixed 20s transport timeout. Merged selector/batch work and dispatcher-path error handling mitigate adjacent cases, but they do not settle the upload contract or prove the reported dynamic-page flow fixed.

## What This Changes

The issue asks for browser upload/file-chooser flows to prefer atomic upload-with-trigger behavior and keep stale-ref/action timeouts specific instead of reporting browser-control outages.

## Best Possible Solution

Make the Browser upload contract explicit: preserve paths-only arming with clearer timeout/error semantics, or steer agent/tool flows to atomic upload-with-trigger behavior, then prove it with focused tests and a live dynamic-page upload trace.
