Rationale: Current behavior intentionally exposes fallback transitions for observability, while this PR changes that default based solely on chat type; source inspection cannot determine which shared rooms are trusted operator surfaces or whether direct external chats must also suppress the diagnostics.

Options:

- **Use a surface-aware policy (recommended):** Preserve lifecycle events everywhere and deliver notices only on explicitly private or operator-facing surfaces rather than treating `ChatType` as the complete trust boundary.

- **Keep current visibility:** Do not merge the suppression behavior and continue relying on existing source-suppression metadata and operational guidance.
