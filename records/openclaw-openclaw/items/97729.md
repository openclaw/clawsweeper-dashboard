Keep the structured unsupported-capability error, but make capable exec-approval nodes pass the scoped RPC first, either by checking the correct declared capability directly or by landing the command-advertisement surface with matching proof.

## Solution Assessment

No. Gateway-boundary validation is the right layer for the raw TypeError, but this implementation gates the scoped exec-approvals RPC on the wrong filtered command surface and can reject capable nodes.
