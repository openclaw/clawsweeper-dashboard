## What This Changes

The PR batches pending generated-media session keys once per expired cron-continuation reaper sweep, replacing repeated per-session task-registry scans with `Set.has()` lookups.

## Best Possible Solution

Merge the exact reviewed head after the normal required checks complete, retaining the lazy per-sweep snapshot and its focused contract coverage.
