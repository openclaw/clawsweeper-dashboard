Options:

- **Diagnostic plus explicit reset (recommended):** Status or doctor reports file health and points to a backup-first reset command, keeping destructive action operator-controlled.
- **Diagnostic only:** Status or doctor reports corrupt or oversized state and safe manual steps while leaving reset automation for a later issue.
- **Automatic quarantine:** The gateway moves bad state aside and recreates state on load, maximizing recovery but taking the largest compatibility and data-continuity risk.

Yes for the diagnostic and recovery direction, but the exact reset/quarantine semantics need maintainer choice before implementation.
