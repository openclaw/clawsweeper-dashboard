  - body: `hasSession` only checks the generated `ProcessSession.id`, but `runExecProcess` records supervisor ownership as `opts.sessionKey?.trim() || sessionId`. With a non-empty sessionKey, the next reconciliation treats the live run as untracked and cancels it, so legitimate background jobs can be killed during a later exec or process-tool call.
  - confidence: 0.94

  - body: `reason?: TerminationReason` lets callers pass natural terminal reasons like `exit`, `signal`, or `spawn-error` into a forced cancellation path. Narrow this to cancellation/timeout reasons or remove the option until there is a real caller so killed orphans cannot be recorded as natural exits.
