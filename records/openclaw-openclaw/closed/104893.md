## What This Changes

The PR retains draft-preview ownership when deletion fails, compare-and-clears it after successful deletion, and retries Discord cleanup once during teardown after a fresh final reply.

Make successful deletion the canonical ownership-release point, retain compare-and-clear concurrency safety, keep Discord’s single lifecycle-bound retry, and explicitly establish this behavior as the supported channel-outbound SDK contract before merge.
