  - body: The new `pendingCatchupDeferral` marker is persisted inside `job.state`, but only list/read/listPage are sanitized. `cron.update` responds with the raw job returned by `updateWithPrecondition`, and declarative `cron.add` convergence can also pass a raw pending-deferral job through `cronJobReadView`, whose spread preserves `state`.
