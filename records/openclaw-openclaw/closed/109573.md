## Summary

Keep open: the proposed bounds prevent cache growth, but once either cache is full they permanently suppress warnings for every new account or chat, contradicting the stated fallback behavior; the PR also lacks real-runtime proof.

## What This Changes

Caps the iMessage startup and per-chat group-allowlist warning deduplication sets at 64 and 512 entries respectively.

## Best Possible Solution

Use bounded FIFO/LRU eviction—or an existing bounded-cache helper—so an unseen key evicts an older key, is recorded, and still emits its warning; cover both caches at capacity and cap-plus-one, then provide redacted live log proof.
