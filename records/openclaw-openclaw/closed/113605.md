## What This Changes

The PR gives the Slack live-QA observer a short request timeout, disables its automatic rate-limit retries, and aborts its local retry backoff during teardown so completed scenarios do not remain blocked in observer polling.

## System Context

QA Lab runs live Slack scenarios through a transport adapter that sends scenario traffic and separately polls Slack for observed messages. After scenario execution ends, adapter cleanup awaits that observer; this change aims to ensure rate limits or slow Slack reads cannot hold the entire QA run open.
