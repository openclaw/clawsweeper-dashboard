## Summary

Keep this maintainer-owned PR open, but do not merge the current head: the task-flow restore guard runs too late to prevent partial effects in linked-task cancellation, notification delivery, and scheduled maintenance.

## What This Changes

The PR makes task-flow restoration atomic and sticky on failure, rejects linked task mutations and maintenance while restoration is failed, preserves audit diagnostics, and reloads task-flow state during in-process gateway restart.

## Best Possible Solution

Preflight task-flow readiness at the beginning of every owner action for an existing linked task—cancellation, delivery, and scheduled maintenance—before external effects or durable writes, while retaining record-level guards and allowing standalone tasks to operate independently.
