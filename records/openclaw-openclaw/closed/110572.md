## Summary

Keep open: the report has a concrete deterministic QA reproduction in which the child task is succeeded and delivered but the requester never emits its required synthesis. The merged diagnostics PR at https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/110574 made that distinction observable; it did not change the requester wake path. Current main still treats successful direct delivery as a completed dispatch outcome, so the durable owner for reconciling an already-settled child with the requester turn needs an explicit maintainer choice. The `maintainer` label also prevents automated cleanup.

Likely related people: Takhoffman is the recent contributor on the dispatch failure path; steipete is connected through the original delivery-pipeline refactor and the merged QA diagnostics.

## What This Changes

The issue requests a fix so a requester session reliably resumes and produces its final answer after a direct child subagent succeeds and its completion is delivered.

## Best Possible Solution

Make the canonical persisted child-task state idempotently drive or reconcile the requester continuation, then cover both normal delivery and the race where the child settles before the requester wait is registered.
