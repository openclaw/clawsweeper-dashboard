## What This Changes

The PR stages authorized local outbound media into queue-owned process-generation artifacts, replays those copies after producer death, and reclaims them after terminal queue transitions or proven owner death.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep queue-owned replay copies as the canonical durability boundary, never persist sensitive or unauthorized media, reclaim only after durable terminal state or proven owner death, and add sufficient operator visibility for retained spool storage.
