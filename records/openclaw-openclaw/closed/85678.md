## Best Possible Solution

Keep the low-latency cue only if the worker receives a canonical authorization decision or equivalent safe snapshot, and route the action through a worker-compatible form of the shared Telegram request guard with coverage for authorized, pairing-pending, blocked, disabled, 401, and thread-target cases.
