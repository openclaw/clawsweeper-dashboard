## System Context

The native hook relay connects provider-native hook processes, currently Codex, to OpenClaw’s agent hook and approval system. It accepts hook payloads through a loopback bridge, normalizes them into relay events, applies OpenClaw policy and approvals, and returns provider-specific hook responses.
