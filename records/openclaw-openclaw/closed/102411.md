Full review comments:

- **[P2] Anchor the base64 marker before the payload:** `src/gateway/managed-image-attachments.ts:284`
  - body: `lastIndexOf(";base64,")` searches the whole string after `data:`, including text after an earlier comma. Split at the first comma and validate the metadata before the payload, or require the marker to occur before the first comma.
