## Summary

Keep this issue open. Current main supports `/learn` from the active conversation or named sources and current-turn Skill Workshop capture, but it does not provide incremental older-session discovery, persisted coverage, or the proposed UI flow. The protected `maintainer` label and unresolved privacy, cost, eligibility, cursor, and UX policy require explicit product direction.

**Likely related people**
- `steipete` — high confidence; introduced the experimental Skill Workshop and owns this product proposal.
- `vincentkoc` — high confidence; recently maintained its capture-hook and live-config behavior.

## What This Changes

Add operator-triggered backward scanning of eligible past sessions to Skill Workshop, with bounded batches, a persisted per-agent cursor, pending-only proposals, and coverage UI.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep the capability Workshop-owned: expose a generic bounded session-query seam, persist each agent's coverage cursor in its canonical SQLite database, provide explicit scan and reset controls with cost/privacy disclosure, and route every result through the existing pending proposal and safety pipeline.
