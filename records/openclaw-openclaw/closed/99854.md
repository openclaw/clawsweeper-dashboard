## What This Changes

The issue reports that Telegram direct mentions in always-on group ingress can be stamped as `room_event`, making `message_tool_only` turns lurk and keep final replies private instead of sending to chat.

## Best Possible Solution

Trace the ambient/isolated Telegram ingress path and make the resolved addressed/mention decision the single fact used for classification, prompt context, and delivery directives, with a regression test that makes `ExplicitlyMentionedBot: true` plus `InboundEventKind: room_event` impossible.

## Reproduction Assessment

No full end-to-end current-main reproduction was established in this read-only pass. Source inspection confirms the harmful branch once a direct mention is stamped `room_event`, and the issue body supplies production evidence for that divergent state.

## Solution Assessment

Yes directionally: the best fix is to carry one canonical addressed/mention fact through Telegram ingress into classification and prompt context. A prompt-only belt-and-suspenders change may help, but the primary repair should be the classification/wiring divergence.
