- **[P2] Preserve same-line separators before heredoc bodies:** `src/agents/tool-display-exec-shell.ts:359`
  - body: This skips from the `<<` operator to the terminator before visiting the rest of the heredoc opener line. Current main avoids the reported footer leak by stripping heredoc bodies in the exec-summary layer instead.
