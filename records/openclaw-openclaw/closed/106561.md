## Summary

## What This Changes

Ensure aborting a foreground bash tool promptly settles its execution promise even when the spawned process does not terminate.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep cancellation and force-termination ownership centralized in the process supervisor; reopen the canonical design only with a concrete current-main reproduction showing that supervisor cancellation never settles despite its termination escalation.
