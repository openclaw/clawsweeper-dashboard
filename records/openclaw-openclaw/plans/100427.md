## Plan

Fix the blocking policy hook model-identity gap from https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/100427. Keep the solution narrow: do not add a new public hook or config option. Confirm `model_call_ended` behavior stays unchanged. Do not implement the longer-term provider hook pair unless maintainers separately approve that SDK surface.
