# Coding Plan for openclaw/openclaw#103720: [Bug]: Deleted transcripts ingested by dreaming + incomplete heartbeat filter leaks heartbeat noise into dream corpus

Source report: [records/openclaw-openclaw/items/103720.md](records/openclaw-openclaw/items/103720.md)

## Summary

This issue remains necessary on current main. Archived transcripts are intentionally retained for memory search, but Dreaming consumes the same corpus metadata without a separate eligibility or deduplication rule; the sanitizer also drops only the exact `HEARTBEAT_OK` token, so generated natural-language heartbeat replies can remain. The two suggested one-line changes are not safe as written, but the underlying Dreaming corpus-contamination bug is actionable and is not fully owned by the related issues or their closed PRs.

## Plan

Fix the Dreaming corpus contamination described by https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/103720. Preserve reset/deleted transcript visibility for `memory_search`; do not remove archives from the shared session-file or transcript-corpus discovery path. Introduce the narrowest metadata-owned way for Dreaming to exclude or deduplicate archive artifacts without losing unique archived history. Also prevent assistant replies generated for `[OpenClaw heartbeat poll]` turns from entering the Dreaming corpus by carrying reliable turn provenance across parsing; do not broadly reject short assistant messages, and do not assume `isHeartbeatOkResponse` catches acknowledgments without `HEARTBEAT_OK`. Add focused regression tests for active-plus-archive duplication, orphan archives, exact-token heartbeat replies, natural-language generated heartbeat replies, and ordinary assistant replies that must remain. Keep broader configurable exclusions in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/72611 and process-chatter filtering in https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/issues/80664 out of scope. Do not edit `CHANGELOG.md`; include user-visible release-note context in the PR body.
