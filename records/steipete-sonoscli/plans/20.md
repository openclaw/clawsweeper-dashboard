On current main, `SMAPIClient.CompleteAuthentication` and `FileSMAPITokenStore.Save` reject credentials unless both `authToken` and `privateKey` are non-empty, but Sonos documents `privateKey` as refresh-only and a token-only `getDeviceAuthToken` response as valid.
