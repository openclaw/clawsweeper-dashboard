Keep open. ## What This Changes

The issue requests a one-time invalidation and retry of a stale cached OAuth client registration when dynamic client registration fails.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a narrowly scoped, single-retry DCR recovery path that invalidates only a cached client registration after a confirmed `registerClient` failure, then prove both successful recovery and preservation of non-DCR or transient-error behavior.
