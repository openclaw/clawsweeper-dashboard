## Summary

Current main and v0.23.1 do not implement the requested public, multidimensional quality signals. Existing quality machinery is an internal anti-spam publishing gate, while discovery ranking uses adoption and freshness; the remaining scoring authority, explainability, appeals, and discovery effects require maintainer product direction.

## What This Changes

Evaluate adding public, multidimensional quality signals for ClawHub skills beyond existing security indicators.

## Best Possible Solution

Specify and pilot separate, explainable quality dimensions with provenance and freshness, keeping them advisory until maintainers define correction, appeal, and discovery effects; only then consider a composite score.
