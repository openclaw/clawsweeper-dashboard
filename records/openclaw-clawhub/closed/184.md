## Summary

Current main and v0.23.1 provide Official publisher identity, artifact provenance tiers, automated scanning, and moderation review, but not a paid expedited human-review certification program. The broader reviewed-community RFC does not settle the payment question, so this issue retains a meaningful product and security decision.

## What This Changes

Add a paid expedited human security-review process and approval badge for skills that pass manual review.

## Best Possible Solution

Resolve the reviewed-community trust model in the RFC, then separately decide whether a fee may purchase queue priority; any artifact attestation should be auditable, expiring, revocable, and clearly separate from Official identity, provenance tiers, and automated scans.
