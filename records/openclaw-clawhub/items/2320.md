## Summary

Keep open. Current main provides public claim intake and guarded account/publisher recovery, but it still leaves evidence thresholds, disputed-ownership principles, deleted-owner treatment, and public recovery guarantees to explicit security and product judgment; this collaborator-authored RFC therefore remains necessary.

## What This Changes

Define transparent policy for organization and namespace claim disputes and for account or publisher recovery after owner-initiated deletion.

## Best Possible Solution

Approve durable adjudication invariants in the security and identity specs, including evidence classes, continuity requirements, deleted-owner treatment, possible outcomes, appeals, and fail-closed exceptions, then publish the safe user-facing guarantees while retaining sensitive verification details in a private staff runbook.
