## Summary

Keep open. Current main has implemented public namespace-claim intake and guarded recovery work, but this collaborator-authored RFC still needs an explicit maintainer-approved security and product contract for evidence thresholds, disputed ownership, deleted-owner treatment, and what recovery outcomes can be publicly promised.

## What This Changes

Define transparent policy for organization and namespace claim disputes plus account recovery after an owner deletes their account.

## Best Possible Solution

Approve a concise public claims-and-recovery policy that states acceptable public evidence, private-review boundaries, decision outcomes, and the distinction between eligible self-delete recovery and ban or security enforcement; retain the detailed reviewer runbook in internal specifications.
