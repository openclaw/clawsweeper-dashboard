## Summary

Keep open: current main and v0.23.1 provide an internal publish-time quality gate, popularity/freshness metadata, and security audits, but they do not provide the requested public multidimensional skill-quality signals. The related open discussions overlap without superseding this broader dataset-backed proposal.

## What This Changes

Add public multidimensional quality signals for skills, covering documentation, maintenance, claim accuracy, complexity, dependencies, and alternatives separately from security verdicts.

## Best Possible Solution

Define independently sourced, explainable quality dimensions in a durable spec, pilot them as advisory detail-page information, and leave ranking, security verdicts, and installability unchanged until calibration and appeal policy are proven.
