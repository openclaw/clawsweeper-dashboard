## Summary

Keep open: current main has strong adjacent acceptable-use and agent-facing audit guidance, but it still does not explicitly decide the full acceptability boundary requested by this RFC or whether declared capabilities change treatment. The remaining work is an active security-policy choice rather than an already-implemented documentation cleanup.

## What This Changes

Establish a documented ClawHub policy distinguishing acceptable task-scoped skill instructions from agent-directed instructions that require review or are disallowed.

## Best Possible Solution

After maintainer approval, record the durable moderation invariant in `specs/security-moderation.md` and add matching allowed, review-required, and disallowed examples to `docs/acceptable-usage.md`, with declarations affecting review context but not authorizing prohibited behavior.
