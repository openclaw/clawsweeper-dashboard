## Summary

## What This Changes

The issue requests a documented policy distinguishing acceptable task-scoped skill instructions from unacceptable agent-directed instructions that fetch/run external code, escalate authority, bypass confirmation, or exceed the skill purpose.

## Best Possible Solution

Have maintainers/security reviewers decide the acceptability boundary, then capture durable intent in a security/moderation spec and public publisher/moderator guidance in acceptable-use or security-audit docs.
