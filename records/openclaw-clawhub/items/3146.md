## Summary

Keep open: the reporter reproduced a live registry inconsistency on July 17, 2026 with fresh skill and plugin publishes, while current main still has separate reservation, finalization, moderation, and visibility stages that could strand a version before it becomes owner-inspectable. The related discovery reports concern already-readable releases and do not cover this publish-finalization failure.

## What This Changes

Investigate and safely remediate published skill and plugin versions that are reserved after publish but remain unavailable to inspect, discover, or install.

## Best Possible Solution

Make an accepted publish atomically owner-inspectable, expose its finalization and moderation state by publish ID, and add a guarded administrator repair that either completes or safely rolls back stranded records after verifying ownership, artifacts, and scan results.
