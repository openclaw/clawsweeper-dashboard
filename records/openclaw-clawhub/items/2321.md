## Summary

Keep this open: current main implements behavior-based spam, malicious-content, visibility, install, and account-enforcement rules, but it does not resolve the RFC’s category-wide financial-content boundary. The collaborator-authored RFC also has active product and security review signals, so explicit maintainer direction is still required.

## What This Changes

Define ClawHub policy categories for spam, financial content, malicious artifacts, visibility, installability, human review, and account enforcement.

## Best Possible Solution

Record a behavior-based category matrix in `specs/security-moderation.md`, publish its user-facing eligibility and enforcement rules in `docs/acceptable-usage.md`, and keep detection thresholds and reviewer tactics private.

Options:

- **Behavior-Based Boundary (recommended):** Disallow deceptive, unsafe, or unapproved financial actions while permitting clearly described lower-risk identity, registry, reputation, educational, and read-only uses.
- **Category-Wide Prohibition:** Exclude crypto, blockchain, finance, and trading listings from the public registry regardless of narrower behavior or safeguards.
- **Default-Deny Exceptions:** Hold these categories for human review and enumerate narrow permitted cases in public policy.
