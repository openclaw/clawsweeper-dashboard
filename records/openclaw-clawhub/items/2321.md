## Summary

Keep open: this collaborator-authored moderation/security RFC still needs an explicit maintainer product and security decision. Current main provides behavior-based acceptable-use rules and moderation tooling, but it does not settle the requested category-wide financial/crypto boundary or a public category-to-enforcement mapping.

## What This Changes

The RFC requests public content-policy categories and corresponding discovery, review, installability, and account-enforcement treatment for ClawHub uploads.

## Best Possible Solution

Approve a concise public policy matrix that separates harmful behavior and transaction-authorizing tools from contextual or non-transactional content, maps each approved category to visibility/installability/review/enforcement outcomes, and keeps scanner tactics and reviewer-only signals in the moderation spec.

Options:

- **Adopt behavior-based categories (recommended):** Publish a matrix that restricts transaction-authorizing, wallet, trading, and abusive behaviors while reviewing non-transactional content on its actual capabilities and disclosures.
- **Adopt a broad financial-content restriction:** Exclude crypto, blockchain, finance, and trading content from the public registry, with any narrow exceptions written and reviewed explicitly.
- **Defer category policy:** Retain the current outcome-based acceptable-use policy and reviewer discretion until maintainers have a broader content-policy proposal.
