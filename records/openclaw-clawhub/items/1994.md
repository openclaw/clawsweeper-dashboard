## Summary

Keep open under the ClawHub-specific cleanup policy. Current main and v0.23.1 distinguish admin-controlled Official status from artifact verification and provenance tiers, but neither provides a public community verification request path; the product and security decision remains open in the canonical RFC at https://github.com/openclaw/clawhub/issues/2272.

## What This Changes

Clarify how a published community skill or plugin can obtain a verified trust designation on ClawHub.

## Best Possible Solution

Resolve the canonical RFC with explicit official-only or vetted-community semantics, then publish one consistent eligibility, application, review, renewal, revocation, and appeal process that keeps automated scan and provenance evidence distinct from human endorsement.
