## What This Changes

The issue asks ClawHub to enforce canonical casing for `SKILL.md` and `PROTOCOL.md`, or at least reject case-colliding uppercase/lowercase duplicates in skill packages.

## Best Possible Solution

Maintainers should choose the casing policy, likely preserve documented lowercase aliases while rejecting case-colliding duplicates, decide whether `PROTOCOL.md` belongs in the skill contract, then update specs, docs, validation, and tests consistently.
