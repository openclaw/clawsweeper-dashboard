## What This Changes

## Best Possible Solution

Adopt the exact-release scanner only after maintainers approve the OSV-to-trust policy and a real Convex deployment proves clean, advisory, malware, outage/retry, backfill, and post-scan manual-restore behavior without affecting older clean releases.
