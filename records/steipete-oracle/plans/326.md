## Plan

Fix https://github.com/steipete/oracle/issues/326 by extending the existing browser recovery machinery to handle a target-level CDP client disconnect while Chrome and the page target remain alive. In `src/browser/index.ts`, distinguish a transient client/WebSocket disconnect from actual browser or target closure by probing the recorded CDP endpoint and re-resolving the target or saved conversation.
