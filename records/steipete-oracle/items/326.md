## Label Justifications

- `P1`: A successful long-running browser workflow can lose its completed answer and remain indefinitely marked running after a recoverable transport disconnect.
- `impact:message-loss`: The completed ChatGPT response exists server-side but Oracle fails to capture or persist it for the user.

## Summary

Keep open: the reviewed main snapshot still misclassifies a target-level CDP disconnect as a closed Chrome window and does not auto-reattach from the resulting `connection-lost` path. The specifically linked implementation at https://github.com/steipete/oracle/pull/327 remains the active resolution path and must stay paired with this issue until it merges or is otherwise resolved.

## What This Changes

Recover completed browser-mode answers by reconnecting to a still-live CDP target after a target-client disconnect and reconciling the session to completed.

## Best Possible Solution

Land a narrowly validated recovery path that probes browser and target liveness, reconnects to the recorded target or saved conversation, preserves the original disconnect diagnosis, and proves completion reconciliation with a forced-disconnect Pro browser smoke that never uses “Answer now.”
