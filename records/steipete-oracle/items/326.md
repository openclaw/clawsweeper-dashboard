## What This Changes

Recover completed browser-mode answers when the target-level CDP client disconnects but Chrome and the ChatGPT target remain alive.

## Best Possible Solution

Land a narrow recovery path that probes Chrome and the recorded target, resumes once when the disconnect is recoverable, preserves manual reattach fallback on failure, and proves a forced long-Pro disconnect recovers the final answer and completed session state without clicking “Answer now.”
