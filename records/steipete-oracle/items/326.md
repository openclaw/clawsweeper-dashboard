## Summary

Keep open: current main still converts any target-level CDP client disconnect into a “Chrome window closed” error, while the session layer preserves the run for later reattach rather than reconnecting to the still-live target. The existing URL monitor and reattach helpers address adjacent recovery cases but do not establish recovery from this reported connection-loss path.

Likely related people: @LeoLin990405 with high confidence from the recent remote-session recovery work, and Peter Steinberger with medium confidence from broader browser-pipeline maintenance.

## What This Changes

Recover browser-mode runs from a target-level CDP disconnect by reconnecting to the live target, retaining conversation identity, harvesting the completed answer, and updating the session to completed.

## Best Possible Solution

Reuse the existing target-resolution, conversation-monitor, reattach, and harvest machinery to probe the browser and original target after a disconnect, reconnect when it is still live, and atomically persist the recovered conversation and completed response while preserving the underlying disconnect reason.

## Repair Work Prompt

Fix the recoverable target-level CDP disconnect described by this issue. In `src/browser/index.ts`, distinguish a disconnected client from a closed browser or target by probing the recorded CDP endpoint and resolving the original target or saved conversation. Reuse `src/browser/reattach.ts` and `src/browser/conversationUrlMonitor.ts` rather than introducing a parallel recovery system. If the target remains live, reconnect and continue response capture or harvest; if it is gone, preserve the genuine terminal reason. Persist conversation identity as early as available, reconcile a recovered answer atomically to completed session state, and prevent late monitors or competing recovery attempts from overwriting terminal metadata. Add regression tests for live-target reconnect, genuinely closed target, missing conversation URL fallback, and completed-state reconciliation. Include a user-visible changelog entry following Oracle's existing grouping policy. Do not click or automate ChatGPT's “Answer now” control, weaken completion proof, or change unrelated browser timeout and model-selection behavior.
