- `P1`: A completed Deep Research browser run can leave users with junk output and no normal `session --render` recovery, breaking a real long-running browser workflow.
- `impact:message-loss`: The reported failure stores and renders the wrapper text instead of the completed Deep Research report.

## Summary

Keep open: current main still only treats exact tool-only Deep Research captures with tiny output token counts as recoverable, so the reported structured wrapper variant would fall through to saved-log replay instead of completed-session reattach.

## What This Changes

Detect ChatGPT's “Deep Research App” tool-call wrapper as an incomplete Deep Research capture so `oracle session <id> --render` live-reattaches and replaces it with the real report.

## Best Possible Solution

Broaden the shared Deep Research incomplete-capture detection to recognize structured tool-call wrappers, then add focused `attachSession` and Deep Research regression coverage without adding a new flag or rerunning the research.
