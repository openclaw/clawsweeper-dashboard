## Risks / Open Questions

- When a helper resolves, existing POSIX bridges move into a detached process group, which changes inherited process-group, SIGHUP, and controlling-terminal behavior for existing installations.

- The design intentionally degrades to the old leak-prone behavior when no helper can be resolved or compiled, and a helper that exits immediately leaves abrupt-death protection unavailable.
- A small interval remains between bridge spawn and watchdog spawn during which immediate owner death can still orphan the bridge tree.
- The current head conflicts with main, so its existing green checks and proof do not validate the eventual three-way merge or rebased implementation.
