- **[P1] Stop capture when IPC accepts the first request:** `src/cli/session/queue-owner-runtime.ts:539`
  - body: `submitToRunningOwner` can wait for the queued task to finish, so calling `stopStartupCapture()` only after it resolves keeps stderr capture active throughout a long-running prompt. Stop retaining diagnostic bytes at the first successful IPC acknowledgement rather than at task completion.
  - confidence: 0.98
- **[P1] Drain post-bind stderr instead of closing the pipe:** `src/cli/session/queue-owner-process.ts:327`
  - body: Destroying the parent's stderr stream closes the pipe while the detached owner remains alive, so a later `process.stderr.write()` can receive `EPIPE` and terminate or destabilize the owner. Disable retention but continue draining and discarding the stream for the child's lifetime.
  - confidence: 0.97
