## Summary

The previous blocking finding remains on the unchanged PR head: after startup capture stops, the queue-owner stderr pipe is drained but still referenced, so the submitting process may remain alive until the detached owner exits. The diagnostic design and proof are otherwise strong, but this lifecycle regression should be fixed before merge.

## What This Changes

The PR adds bounded cold-start queue-owner stderr and exit diagnostics, clearer queue-directory errors, an IPC-acceptance callback that ends retained capture, and focused lifecycle tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Preserve the bounded startup diagnostics and continued drain behavior, but unreference the stderr stream after retained capture ends and prove the submitter exits while the owner stays alive.
