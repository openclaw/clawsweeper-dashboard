## Summary

## What This Changes

Prevent Ultra-mode Terra and Sol forked-context replay from inflating local Pi token usage and cost estimates.

## Best Possible Solution

Land a lineage-aware Pi accounting fix that reparses affected cached files and is validated against a sanitized Ultra replay fixture plus ordinary small, large, lineage-free, and appended-session cases.
