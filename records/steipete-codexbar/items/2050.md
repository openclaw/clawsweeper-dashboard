## Summary

The current head is a focused and apparently correct fix for a deterministic unbounded metadata pre-scan, with credible after-fix large-file timing and peak-memory evidence. It should remain open for normal merge review; it does not establish that the separate catastrophic OOM report is fixed.

## What This Changes

The PR caps Codex session-metadata records at 256 KiB, drains each 64 KiB read in an autorelease pool, recovers after oversized or malformed JSONL records, adds boundary and 8 MiB regression tests, regenerates the parser hash, and adds a changelog note.

## Best Possible Solution

Land the bounded streaming pre-scan with its recovery and exact-boundary coverage, while keeping https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/issues/1999 open for the process-level diagnostics needed to identify its root cause.

## Solution Assessment

Yes. A streaming retained-line cap with newline resynchronization and chunk-scoped autorelease pools fixes the unbounded parser behavior narrowly while preserving cancellation, exact-limit records, later-record recovery, and EOF parsing.
