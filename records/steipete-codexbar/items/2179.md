## What This Changes

The PR makes explicit 4-, 5-, and 7-day Codex schedules override historical pace predictions, renames the nil schedule to Automatic, and updates settings copy, documentation, and focused tests.

## Best Possible Solution

Keep explicit schedules authoritative, reserve learned historical forecasting for Automatic, continue collecting history in every mode, and demonstrate both fresh-selection and existing-preference upgrade behavior in a freshly built CodexBar bundle.
