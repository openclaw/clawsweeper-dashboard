## Summary

The reported cancellation delay is real, but the patch does not consistently preserve cancellation at the public `verifyClick` boundary. A nonthrowing fallback can return an empty result, continue through later verification stages, and—when OCR fallback is disabled—surface `PeekabooError.operationError` instead of `CancellationError`; this needs a focused repair and proof before merge.

Likely related people: Peter Steinberger (high confidence), who introduced the verifier and adjacent tests in the v3.9.0 release commit.

## What This Changes

The PR adds `Task.isCancelled` checks after seven swallowed polling sleeps in `MenuBarClickVerifier`, returning empty results from nonthrowing waiters and throwing cancellation from the popover waiter.

## Best Possible Solution

Propagate `CancellationError` at the async-throwing `verifyClick` boundary after each nonthrowing fallback, or make those waiters throwing, then add deterministic tests for OCR-enabled and OCR-disabled cancellation paths.
