Publish an `axorcist` formula through `openclaw/homebrew-tap`, keep it synchronized with tagged AXorcist releases, document `brew install openclaw/tap/axorcist` plus the manual fallback, and smoke-test the installed `axorc` CLI without requiring Accessibility permission.
