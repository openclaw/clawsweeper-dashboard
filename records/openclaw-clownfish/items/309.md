## Summary

Keep open for maintainer review: the focused patch appears correct, but it intentionally relaxes an autonomous duplicate-close guard and the member-authored draft needs an explicit safety-policy decision before merge.

## What This Changes

The PR makes issue-only imported clusters eligible for duplicate closure without a prior fix while retaining fix-first behavior for eligible PR-containing clusters and security-sensitive clusters.

## Best Possible Solution

Retain the narrow issue-only exception and existing security/PR gates, then confirm it with one bounded duplicate-only canary before enabling the relaxed policy broadly.
